What a survey actually changes

Three things, in the order they matter to the person paying. Not a feature list: every line below is something different about your week, not something inside the service.

  • Cost

    One price, agreed before we start

    The survey finds what the quote has to cover, so the number you accept is the number you pay. Variations need your signature, not a phone call afterwards.

    Fixed-price on 94 of the last 100 jobs.

  • Time

    Two days on site, not two weeks of visits

    Repairs get scheduled as one block once materials are on site. You lose access to the roof for a known window rather than an open one.

  • After

    A written record of what was found

    Photographs of the deck before it was covered, the moisture readings, and what we expect to need attention in five years. Useful when you sell.
