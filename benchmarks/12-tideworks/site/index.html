    Board conventions

    • Closing means under two hours of window left: half the four-hour look-ahead this board is set to. It is a convention of the board, not a fact about the tide.
    • Sill depths are at mean low water. That is a datum, not today's depth. Where a draught is under the MLW figure the boat is clear at any state of tide, and the board says so. Where it is over, the board says how much water is needed and stops: it holds no tide heights. Sound the sill, or work her close to high water.
    • Priority is commercial with a timed cargo, then commercial, then leisure by waiting time. It is the order boats are worked and not a promise about the tide. Salter's Lode shuts at 05:42 and Denver runs to 09:12, and those two facts sit next to each other on purpose.
    • Welches Dam has been out of service since 2006. It stays on the board because leaving it off makes people ask.
    • The log is a record. A wrong line is struck in the book and re-entered; nothing here removes one.

    Every figure on this board is either today's high water at Denver, the standing order's window for a lock, a surveyed sill depth at mean low water, or a boat's declared draught. Nothing on it is computed from a height of tide, because the board holds none.
