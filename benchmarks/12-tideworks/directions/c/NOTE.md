# Direction C — master and detail

The argument: the keeper works one boat at a time, so the screen is a queue on the left and the
selected boat's lock on the right, split down a hard vertical rule. The detail pane carries a
drawn long-section of the lock — chamber, sill, water — with the boat's draught marked against
it, so the go/no-go is a picture rather than a subtraction.

- composition: a split down a hard vertical rule, priority queue left, one lock's detail right
- type: a condensed grotesque over a system sans
- colour: slate ground, one amber for the selected item
- imagery: diagram-led, a drawn lock long-section at plate scale in the detail pane
- rhythm: two fields divided by a vertical rule, the detail set in cards
