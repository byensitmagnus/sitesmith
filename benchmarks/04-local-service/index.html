Prices are what these jobs usually land at. Yours will be quoted after we have seen it, in writing, before anything starts.

    £180–£340usually same week

How we quote

  • →We get on the roof. No quote is given from the ground or from a photo.
  • →You get the price in writing, itemised, with the membrane and the guarantee named.
  • →The price does not change unless we find rotten deck — and then we stop, photograph it, and ring you before continuing.
  • →No deposit on jobs under £1,000. Nothing paid up front for a repair.

When we say no

  • →Pitched tile roofs. We do not do them and we will pass you to someone who does.
  • →Anything above three storeys without scaffold access already in place.
  • →Jobs where a replacement is being asked for and a repair would genuinely do. We will say so and quote the repair.
  • →Emergency call-outs in high wind. Nobody goes on a wet flat roof in a gale.
