Retries and backoff

v3 Updated 22 July 2026 Reading time about 6 minutes

When your endpoint does not accept a delivery, Relay keeps the event and tries again on a fixed schedule. Nothing is dropped silently: an event either succeeds, or ends up in the dead-letter queue where you can see it.

This page covers the schedule itself, what Relay treats as a failure, and how to replay a delivery once your endpoint is healthy again. If you are debugging one specific event, the delivery log on the endpoint page shows every attempt with the response body we saw.

The schedule#

Relay retries eight times over roughly 26 hours. Intervals are exponential with jitter, so a fleet of endpoints recovering from the same outage does not stampede you at the same second.

Note

The schedule is per endpoint, not per event. Pausing an endpoint freezes its timers; resuming continues from the attempt it had reached rather than starting again.

What counts as a failure#

A delivery succeeds on any 2xx. Everything below is retried, except 410 Gone, which disables the endpoint after the first response — it is the documented way to tell us to stop.

  • Connection refused, DNS failure or TLS error — retried.
  • No response within 12 seconds — retried, and the attempt is logged as a timeout.
  • 429 with a Retry-After header — we honour the header when it asks for more than the next scheduled delay, never less.
  • Any other 4xx or 5xx — retried on the schedule above.
  • 3xx — not followed. Point the endpoint at the final URL.
Take care

Returning 200 before you have persisted the event is the most common cause of lost webhooks. Acknowledge after the write, not before it — a retry you never receive cannot be replayed.

Making retries safe

Every attempt carries the same Relay-Event-Id. Store it and reject repeats: that is the whole of idempotency here, and it costs one unique index.

Dead-letter queue#

After the eighth attempt the delivery is dead-lettered. It is kept for 30 days with the full request we sent and the last response we got, and it does not count against your delivery quota.

Replay a single delivery with the call above, or select a time range in the dashboard and replay in bulk. Replays enter the queue as a fresh attempt 1 and follow the same schedule.

  • Bulk replays are rate-limited to 200 deliveries a minute per endpoint.
  • An endpoint disabled by 410 must be re-enabled before anything replays.
  • Deliveries older than 30 days are removed and cannot be recovered.
