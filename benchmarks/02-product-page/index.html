Assembly

What happens between order and dispatch

Parts are checked against the order line by line, assembled, and cable-routed behind the tray. The machine then runs a four-hour combined CPU and GPU load while temperatures and clocks are logged.

If anything throttles outside spec, it does not ship — it goes back to the bench. The log is printed and packed with the machine so you can compare it against your own numbers on day one.
