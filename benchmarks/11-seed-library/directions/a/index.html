Cleeve Seed Library

Take up to six packets of seed. In autumn, bring some back.

A lending library of vegetable seed inside Cleeve Branch Library, Gloucestershire, since 2019. No fee, no deposit, and no penalty if you bring nothing back — which is why we would rather explain the return than enforce it.
