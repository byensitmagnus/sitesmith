# Comp C — the year, as one object

The argument: the loop is the whole design problem, so draw the loop. One object, centred: the
borrowing year as a ring, spring at the top, autumn coming back round, the six-packet limit in
the middle. Everything else is a card beneath it. Two accents, one for the going out and one
for the coming back, because the ring needs two directions to read as a loop.

- composition: a single drawn object centred on the ground, the borrowing year as a ring, everything else beneath it
- type: one system sans throughout, at three weights
- colour: limewash near-white ground, two accents, one for spring and one for autumn
- imagery: object-led, one drawing at plate scale and nothing else
- rhythm: a card grid on a near-white field
