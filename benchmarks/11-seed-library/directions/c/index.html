Cleeve Seed Library

One turn of the year, and the seed comes home

Free vegetable seed inside Cleeve Branch Library, Gloucestershire. Borrow up to six packets in spring; save seed from your best plants and bring some back in autumn.

Free, with no catch to read

No membership fee, no deposit, and no penalty if you return nothing. The branch provides the drawers and the space.

Five easy, four not

Runner bean, pea, tomato, lettuce and French bean can be saved by a beginner. Kale, squash, beetroot and carrot cross with their neighbours.

96 of 412

412 members have registered since 2019. 96 brought seed back in autumn 2025. We think that is low.
