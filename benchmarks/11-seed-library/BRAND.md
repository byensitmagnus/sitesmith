# BRAND — The Cleeve Seed Library

> Inventory only. Nothing is chosen here.

## 1. What exists

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | None. The library has never had one. No vector, no raster, no wordmark. |
| **Colour** | None owned. The brief names no brand colour and implies none. |
| **Type** | None in use. |
| **Photography** | None. Not of the drawers, the library, the volunteers or the seed. |
| **Voice** | Present and strong, and it is the one thing that does exist. From the brief: the volunteers *"consider this low and say so publicly"*. Plain, unpromotional, willing to publish an uncomfortable number. Two windows a week, six packets a visit, no fee, no deposit, no penalty. |

Everything above is **unknown-to-absent** rather than owned-and-fixed. That is a finding, not
a blank: there is no existing identity to design around and no rebrand being imposed on
anyone.

## 2. Fixed and open

**Fixed** (four things, all from the brief, none of them visual):

1. The nine crops, their packet counts and their easy/hard split — the content is the design.
2. The six-packet limit — the shape of the primary action.
3. 96 of 412, unspun.
4. The two allotment associations may be named **in text only**. No mark may be drawn for
   either. This is a fixed constraint on the identity system, not a preference.

**Open:** everything visual.

## 3. Reference board

In `EVIDENCE.md` section 6 — herbarium sheet, library date-due slip, nineteenth-century
seedsman's list; against the DTC seed shop, the community-project page and the garden-centre
landing page.

## 4. What has to be made

Straight into `ASSET-MANIFEST.md`:

- a mark, because the page renders one and none exists;
- a favicon, which cannot be designed out;
- drawings of the nine seeds, because no photograph exists and none can be taken;
- two explanatory drawings (the pollen mechanism, the borrowing year);
- one figure drawing (412 marks, 96 of them filled).

**Nothing here has chosen a colour, a face or an arrangement.** That happens in
`DIRECTION.md`.
