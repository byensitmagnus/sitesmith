# ASSET-PLAN — The Cleeve Seed Library

Written after `DIRECTION.md` and before anything was drawn. Five assets. Every one of them is
**drawn**, because `EVIDENCE.md` §7 says no photograph of this library exists, none can be
taken, and there is no image-generation budget — which leaves drawn, typographic, or designed
out. Imagery is load-bearing here (the borrower's whole decision is *which packets*), so
"designed out" is not available, and one drawing does more than a paragraph could.

The visitor's two jobs, taken from the brief and used verbatim below:

- **J1** — borrow seed this week, and take packets a first-time saver can actually save.
- **J2** — understand what they are promising to do in return, in six months.

---

## `seed-portraits`

- kind: product
- carries: that these are nine different seeds, not nine names on one drawer — and that five of them are drawn solid because the seed you save comes back true, while four are drawn as a broken outline because it will not
- job: J1 — borrow seed this week, and take packets a first-time saver can actually save
- use: drawn once at a 100-unit square, used three times over. All nine at 115px in a single key across the top of the first screen, in one frame, cut by a hard vertical rule into the five and the four. Then at 44px at the head of its own row in the index, so the drawing and the entry are visibly the same object. Then at 16px on each line of the borrowing slip.
- comparative: yes — all nine in one frame at one scale, so the split can be seen before a word is read
- without-it: the easy/hard split collapses to a word in a column, and the brief's one hard constraint — tell easy from hard without reading a paragraph — is unmet
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md §5 "Current stock, by saving difficulty, as at July 2026" — the nine crops, their counts and their difficulty class; drawn in line and ink only per §4, because no seed colour is verified

## `pollen-key`

- kind: proof
- carries: why the split exists at all — pollen that never leaves the flower gives seed that grows the same plant, and pollen that arrives from a mile away gives seed that does not
- job: J1 — borrow seed this week, and take packets a first-time saver can actually save
- use: two drawn panels side by side at the head of the index, each about 200px wide, sharing one baseline; the left panel's arrow curls back into its own flower, the right panel's arrow arrives from off the edge of the frame. Sits directly above the nine rows it explains, not in a separate section.
- comparative: yes — the two mechanisms in one frame, at the same scale, which is the only way "self-pollinating" and "crosses" read as opposites rather than as jargon
- without-it: "hard" is a label a beginner has to take on trust, and a label taken on trust is the thing they ignore when they want the courgette
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md §5, the "why" column of the stock table — self-pollinating; crosses with any brassica within a mile; crosses with any other C. maxima; wind-pollinated, needs isolation; biennial, and crosses with wild carrot

## `borrow-year`

- kind: editorial
- carries: that the gap between taking seed and returning it is six months and one growing season, and that the seed goes back into the drawer it came out of
- job: J2 — understand what they are promising to do in return, in six months
- use: one drawing running the full measure of the "What you are promising" section, left to right — spring, summer, autumn — with the return arrow curving back under the line to the start, in the stamp red reserved for the return. Set above the four plain terms, so the shape is read before the words.
- comparative: no — one process, not a choice between options
- without-it: the return becomes a paragraph promising something six months away, which is a hope; the brief asks for a promise
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md §5 "borrow seed in spring, return seed saved from their best plants in autumn"; no months are drawn because no month is in the verified set

## `returns-2025`

- kind: proof
- carries: the proportion of members who brought seed back — 96 marks filled out of 412 drawn, seen as an area rather than read as a fraction
- job: J2 — understand what they are promising to do in return, in six months
- use: 412 marks on a 28-wide grid, the 96 filled ones grouped at the top left so the filled block can be held against the whole; full measure, directly under the section heading, with the volunteers' own sentence about it set beside rather than beneath
- comparative: no — one quantity against its own whole, which is a proportion and not a choice
- without-it: 96 and 412 are two numerals in a sentence, and the brief's instruction that the rate is neither hidden nor spun is met only in the letter
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md §5 "412 members registered; 96 returned seed in autumn 2025. The volunteers consider this low and say so publicly."

## `mark-czar`

- kind: brand
- carries: that this library's identity is its own holding — the mark is the runner bean 'Czar', the seed in the fullest drawer, drawn in the same hand and the same two states as everything else on the page
- job: J1 — borrow seed this week, and take packets a first-time saver can actually save
- use: 34px beside the wordmark in the masthead and again in the footer, sitting on the same hairline that every seed portrait sits on; reduced to a 32px favicon with the hilum kept and the fuzz dropped
- comparative: no — it is one object, and it is the identity rather than a choice
- without-it: the page has a wordmark and no ident, and the specimen-sheet device that holds the whole design together is missing from the one place a visitor meets it first
- evidence: none — drawn for this project

## What deliberately has no asset

**The two allotment associations.** `EVIDENCE.md` §5 records that Bishop's Cleeve Allotment
Society and Woodmancote Growers Association wrote in March 2026, agreed to be named, and
permit use of their names **in text**. Neither supplied a mark and neither was asked for one.

There is therefore **no `partner-logos` block in this plan and no logo row in the manifest**,
and this is not an omission. Drawing a mark for either would be inventing an endorsement they
did not give: they lent a name, and a name is set in type. `v2/24-asset-plan.md` is explicit
that borrowed marks are never `substitute` and never invented, and that a page with no such
evidence makes its case some other way — so this one does, in a sentence that says who wrote,
when, and that no mark was requested.

**Photography of the drawers, the library and the volunteers.** None exists, none can be
commissioned inside this brief, and a generated stand-in of a place that has never been
photographed would be a picture of a library that does not exist. The nine seeds are the
evidence instead, and they are the thing the visitor actually has to recognise in autumn.
