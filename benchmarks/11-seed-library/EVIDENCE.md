> Everything marked `(inferred)` is reasoning from the real trade, not from this subject, and
> may shape the *drawing* but may never be stated as a fact about this library.

## 2. Vocabulary

**Words this library's people use** — borrow, return, bring back, packet, drawer, saver,
seed-saver, session, member, volunteer, self-pollinating, crosses, cross, isolation, wind-
pollinated, biennial, brassica, *C. maxima*, wild carrot, bolts, sets seed, pod, fermenting,
best plants, comes back true.

**Words the category uses that this library does not** — shop, basket, collection, curated,
premium, exclusive, subscription, sold out, "grow-your-own journey".

The distinction matters because the brief names the failure mode in those exact terms: a
visitor who takes seed without understanding the return *"is not a member, they are a
customer of a free shop"*. Any word from the second list moves the page toward the second
reading.

## 3. Materials and surfaces

`(inferred — the material world of branch libraries and seed-saving generally, not of this
library specifically)`

Manila and buff card. Small paper envelopes. Pencil. Rubber date stamps. Ruled index cards
and the hard hairline rules of a catalogue drawer. Steel or wood drawer fronts with a label
slot. Dry, matte, small objects that are handled with dry fingers. Nothing glossy, nothing
back-lit, nothing photographed.

The implication for the page: **flat**, **ruled**, **square-cornered**, printed rather than
rendered. Paper does not have a drop shadow.

- The buff of manila card and kraft seed envelopes `(inferred)`.
- Graphite `(inferred)`.

- **The seeds' own colours are not recorded anywhere in the brief.** 'Cherokee Trail of

  ink only. Colouring them would be inventing evidence.**

## 5. Constraints already in force

**The verified set, in full — the only facts the page may state.**

- Started 2019.
- Open Tuesdays 14:00–18:00 and Saturdays 10:00–13:00, inside Cleeve Branch Library.
- Closed for two weeks over Christmas.
- 412 members registered; 96 returned seed in autumn 2025. The volunteers consider this low
  and say so publicly.
- Seed is free. No membership fee, no deposit, no penalty for returning nothing.
- Borrowing limit: six packets per visit.
- Current stock, by saving difficulty, as at July 2026:

  | crop | packets | saving difficulty | why |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Runner bean 'Czar' | 61 | easy | self-pollinating, seed obvious in the pod |
  | Pea 'Champion of England' | 44 | easy | self-pollinating |
  | Tomato 'Ailsa Craig' | 38 | easy | needs fermenting, which is one extra step |
  | Lettuce 'Bronze Arrow' | 29 | easy | bolts and sets seed readily |
  | French bean 'Cherokee Trail of Tears' | 22 | easy | self-pollinating |
  | Kale 'Pentland Brig' | 14 | hard | crosses with any brassica within a mile |
  | Squash 'Crown Prince' | 9 | hard | crosses with any other C. maxima |
  | Beetroot 'Cheltenham Green Top' | 7 | hard | wind-pollinated, needs isolation |
  | Carrot 'Autumn King' | 4 | hard | biennial, and crosses with wild carrot |

- Four volunteers: two retired seed-savers, two library staff giving their own time.
- No funding, no donations taken; the branch provides the drawers and the space.
- Two Gloucestershire allotment associations wrote to endorse the library and agreed to be
  named: **Bishop's Cleeve Allotment Society** and **Woodmancote Growers Association**. Both
  letters are on file, dated March 2026, and both permit use of their names **in text**.
  **Neither supplied a logo file and neither was asked for one.**
- No certifications, no awards, no funding bodies.

**Prohibitions carried from the brief.** No invented stock figure, testimonial, funding claim
or endorsement. **No logo may be drawn for either allotment association** — they lent their
names, not marks. The 96-of-412 return rate is not to be hidden or spun; it is the reason the
page exists.

**Technical.** One page, static HTML and CSS, no build step, no framework, no external
requests. Must work at 375, 768 and 1440, in both colour schemes.

**Not in the brief, and therefore absent from the page** `(needed)`: street address,
postcode, telephone number, email address, a named contact, a map, the library's own website,
opening dates for the Christmas closure, and the size in millimetres of any seed.

## 6. References and anti-references

**References** — and precisely what to take from each.

1. **A herbarium specimen sheet.** Take: one object on a plain ground, drawn not photographed,
   with the label block ruled off beneath it and the accession figures at the edge.
2. **A library date-due slip.** Take: the mono stamped figures, the hard rule under the
   heading, and the idea that a date in ink means *this comes back*.
3. **A nineteenth-century seedsman's list** (Suttons, Vilmorin). Take: the cultivar name set
   in italic beside the common name, the count in a right-aligned column, and entries that
   run one after another with a hairline between them and no picture in the gap.

**Anti-references** — what this must not resemble, and why.

1. **The direct-to-consumer seed shop** (pastel packet photography, "shop the collection", a
   basket in the corner). Because it would turn a lending library into exactly the thing the
   brief says kills it: a free shop.
2. **The community-project page** — stock photograph of hands in soil, a large green button,
   a quotation in italics from nobody in particular. Because it says nothing about *these
   nine crops*, and because the invented quotation is precisely what is forbidden here.
3. **The garden-centre "grow your own" landing page** — a hero of a wicker trug and three
   equal feature cards. Because it is the category default, and because a page built that way
   has nowhere honest to put 96 out of 412.
