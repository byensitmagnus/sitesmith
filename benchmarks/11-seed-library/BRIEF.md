# Brief — The Cleeve Seed Library

A public seed library run out of a branch library in Gloucestershire. Members borrow seed in
spring, grow it, and return seed saved from their best plants in autumn. It is free, it is run
by four volunteers, and it lives or dies on whether people bring seed back.

## What the visitor came to do

**A gardener who has never done this needs to borrow seed this week and understand what they
are promising to do in return.**

The whole design problem is the loop. A visitor who takes seed without understanding the return
is not a member, they are a customer of a free shop, and the library empties. So:

1. **Borrow.** What is in the drawers right now, and which of it suits a first-time saver.
   Some crops are easy to save (beans, peas, tomatoes, lettuce). Some cross readily and produce
   nothing like the parent (squash, brassicas, beets). A beginner given a courgette will return
   a mystery, and the library gets worse.
2. **Return.** What happens in six months, in enough detail to be a promise rather than a hope.

## What is true

Every fact below is verified. Nothing outside this list may be stated as fact.

- Started 2019. Open Tuesdays 14:00–18:00 and Saturdays 10:00–13:00, inside Cleeve Branch
  Library, and closed for two weeks over Christmas.
- 412 members registered; 96 returned seed in autumn 2025. The volunteers consider this
  low and say so publicly.
- Seed is free. There is no membership fee, no deposit, and no penalty for returning nothing.
- Borrowing limit: six packets per visit.
- Current stock, by saving difficulty, as at July 2026:

  | crop | packets | saving difficulty | why |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Runner bean 'Czar' | 61 | easy | self-pollinating, seed obvious in the pod |
  | Pea 'Champion of England' | 44 | easy | self-pollinating |
  | Tomato 'Ailsa Craig' | 38 | easy | needs fermenting, which is one extra step |
  | Lettuce 'Bronze Arrow' | 29 | easy | bolts and sets seed readily |
  | French bean 'Cherokee Trail of Tears' | 22 | easy | self-pollinating |
  | Kale 'Pentland Brig' | 14 | hard | crosses with any brassica within a mile |
  | Squash 'Crown Prince' | 9 | hard | crosses with any other C. maxima |
  | Beetroot 'Cheltenham Green Top' | 7 | hard | wind-pollinated, needs isolation |
  | Carrot 'Autumn King' | 4 | hard | biennial, and crosses with wild carrot |

- Four volunteers: two are retired seed-savers, two are library staff giving their own time.
- The library has no funding and takes no donations; the branch provides the drawers and space.
- Two Gloucestershire allotment associations have written to endorse the library and **have
  agreed to be named**: Bishop's Cleeve Allotment Society and Woodmancote Growers Association.
  Both letters are on file, dated March 2026, and both permit use of their names in text.
  Neither has supplied a logo file and neither has been asked for one.
- No certifications, no awards, no funding bodies.

## What must not happen

- No invented stock figure, testimonial, funding claim or endorsement.
- No logo may be drawn for either allotment association. They lent their names, not marks.
- The 96-out-of-412 return rate is not to be hidden or spun. It is the reason the page exists.

## Constraints

- One page, static HTML and CSS. No build step, no framework, no external requests.
- Must work at 375, 768 and 1440, in both colour schemes.
- A beginner must be able to tell easy from hard without reading a paragraph.
