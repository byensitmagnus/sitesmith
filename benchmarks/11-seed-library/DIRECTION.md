# DIRECTION — The Cleeve Seed Library

Three comps under `directions/`, pairwise structurally different on all five axes
(`direction-check.mjs`: PASS). **Comp A, the specimen sheet, wins.**

---

## Axis record

- composition: a drawn key of nine seeds at one scale across the top of the page, split by a hard rule into the ones that come back true and the ones that do not, with the ruled index of the drawers starting immediately beneath it
- type: old-style serif display over the system sans, with every count, time and date set in monospace
- colour: buff paper ground, one stamp red reserved for the return — the promise, the primary action, and the 96 members who kept it
- imagery: diagram-led, ink line drawings of the actual seeds, solid when the seed comes back true and broken when it does not
- rhythm: one continuous paper field, sections separated by a single hard rule and a change of measure

- signature-selector: .seed
- signature-min-share: 6

---

## Why A won

Scored out of the evidence pack, on the five criteria from `v2/20-direction-lab.md` §4.

| criterion | A specimen sheet | B promise set large | C the year as one object |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Comes from the subject | **5** — could not exist for anything but a seed library with nine named crops in two difficulty classes | 3 — the ink-and-gold statement page would fit any campaign with an uncomfortable number | 2 — the ring, the three cards and the centred sub-line would fit any community scheme |
| 2. Serves the primary action | **5** — the nine crops and the easy/hard split are the first thing on the page, which is the borrower's decision | 3 — the stock is two screens down, behind an argument | 3 — the loop is explained beautifully and the drawers are relegated to a card |
| 3. Buildable and maintainable | **5** — every asset is drawn, and drawing is the only route open (no photography exists, no generation budget) | 5 — nothing to make | 4 — one big drawing, and a tenth crop breaks the ring |
| 4. Avoids the anti-references | **5** — a seedsman's list is the opposite of all three | 4 — clears them | **1** — three equal cards on near-white *is* anti-reference 3 |
| 5. One thing worth defending | **5** — the solid/broken seed | 4 — 96/412 at 132px | 3 — the ring |
| **total** | **25** | 19 | 13 |

**The signature, named:** *solid ink means the seed comes back true; a broken outline means it
does not.* One drawing, two states, no colour coding, and it is the answer to the brief's
hardest line — a beginner must tell easy from hard without reading a paragraph. It appears at
115px in the key, at 44px at the head of every index row, at 34px in the mark, and at 16px on
the slip. `signature-selector: .seed`.

**Graft taken from a loser.** From **B**: the 96-of-412 figure set at display scale, in its own
section, with the volunteers' own judgement of it printed next to it. A's list-first structure
would naturally have buried that number in a row of the ledger, which is exactly the spinning
the brief forbids. B proved it wants a room of its own; A gives it one, drawn as 412 marks
rather than set as a numeral, so the proportion is seen and not read.

## Comp B — the promise, set large — rejected

**Axes:** type alone at scale, no imagery · condensed grotesque over system sans · dark ink
ground, one gold accent · deliberately imageless · alternating bands.

**What it did well.** It is the only comp that treats the return as the headline rather than as
a section, and it is right that the library's problem is comprehension, not discovery. The
96/412 at 132px is genuinely arresting, and its graft is in the winner.

**Why it lost, specifically.** The nine crops arrive on the second screen as two sentences of
prose — *"Kale, squash, beetroot and carrot cross with their neighbours"* — and a first-time
saver standing at the drawers on a Tuesday afternoon cannot use a sentence. They have to match
what is in their hand against what is on the page, and B has nothing to match against. It also
inverts the voice: a library whose stated tone is "we consider this low and say so" does not
shout in 96px condensed capitals on black. The brief's failure mode is a visitor who reads the
page as a free shop, and a page that reads as a campaign is a step toward that, not away.

## Comp C — the year, as one object — rejected

**Axes:** a single centred drawn object · one system sans · limewash ground, two accents ·
object-led · card grid.

**What it did well.** The ring is the clearest statement of the loop of the three, and the
six-packet limit sitting inside it is a genuinely good idea — borrowing and returning are one
object, not two sections. The winner keeps the *idea* as a linear drawing (`borrow-year`) and
drops the ring.

**Why it lost, specifically.** It walked straight into anti-reference 3. Three equal cards on a
near-white ground, one of which is headed "96 of 412", turns the number the page exists to
publish into a third of a tidy row — the visual grammar says *these three things are of equal
weight and all of them are fine*, and one of them is not fine. Two accents made it worse: with
a spring green and an autumn orange already in play, the return had no colour left that meant
only the return. And the ring does not scale — it is drawn for a year with three stops, and at
375px it either shrinks below legibility or has to be redrawn as a list, at which point it was
a list all along.

## What the direction commits the build to

- **The ground is buff paper and it is the default scheme.** Dark mode is designed as ink, not
  produced by inversion. The direction has to exist in the scheme a first-time visitor gets.
- **One accent, stamp red, one meaning: the return.** The primary action, the promise lines on
  the slip, and the 96 filled marks. Nowhere else. If it appears on a heading, it is wrong.
- **No photography, ever, and no coloured seed.** Ink line only — the seeds' real colours are
  not in the verified set, and colouring them would be inventing evidence (`EVIDENCE.md` §4).
- **Square corners, no shadows, no cards.** Separation is a rule and a change of measure. The
  page is one sheet.
- **Imagery is load-bearing.** `seed-portraits` carries the borrower's decision. This direction
  does not have the second door open — it is not "deliberately imageless" and may not drift
  into it.
