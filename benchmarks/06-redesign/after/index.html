Rota software for shift-based teams

Publishing the rota is the easy part. It is the next four days that hurt.

ShiftFlow builds the rota against your actual staffing rules, then handles what happens after — the swap requests, the no-shows, the person who cannot legally work a fifth night in a row.

How it works

Four things, in the order they actually happen

  1. 01

    Load your rules once

    Minimum rest between shifts, maximum consecutive nights, who is signed off for which station, and who cannot work Sundays. The rota is generated against those, not around them.

  2. 02

    Build and publish

    Gaps are shown as gaps rather than quietly filled by whoever is cheapest. You publish knowing exactly what is uncovered.

  3. 03

    Swaps go through the rules

    Staff request swaps from their phone. A swap that would break a rest rule is refused with the reason, before it reaches you.

  4. 04

    Hours export to payroll

    Actual worked hours, including the changes, as a file your payroll provider already accepts. No re-keying at month end.

Before you book anything

The four questions we get asked every time

What happens to our existing rota?

Imported from a spreadsheet, including the current week. You do not start from an empty grid on a Monday morning.

Do staff need an account?

They need a phone number. Swap requests and shift confirmations work over SMS if someone will not install anything.

What if the rules are wrong?

Then the rota is wrong, confidently. We set the rules up with you on a call and you sign them off before the first published week.

How do we get our data out?

Full export as CSV at any time, without asking us. Cancelling does not put your history behind a support ticket.

Bring last month’s rota to the call

Forty minutes, your data, your rules. If it cannot handle your rota we will say so on the call rather than in week three.
