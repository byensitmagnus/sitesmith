# DIRECTION.md — Falkner & Vane

Three comps under `directions/`, pairwise structurally different on all five axes, verified by
`direction-check.mjs`. **A wins.**

## Axis record

- composition: the plate first, six hides drawn in section on one shared baseline, starting immediately under a two-line masthead, with no hero image and no statement above it
- type: an old-style serif at plate scale over the same serif for running text, with every measured figure in a tabular monospace
- colour: a buff ground the colour of undyed bark-tanned leather, ink at the strength of oak liquor, and one russet accent reserved for now and for the one thing you do now
- imagery: diagram-led, drawn in ink on the ground, hides in section on one baseline, the yard as a count, the calendar as a measure; nothing on this page came from a camera
- rhythm: one continuous buff field the length of the page, sections divided by a 28-tick tally rule rather than by a change of ground, with the pit yard as the single inset that reverses the palette

- signature-selector: .plate
- signature-min-share: 20

## The signature, in one line

**Six hides drawn edge on, on one baseline, against rules a millimetre apart, at the top of the
page.** You would know a second page of this site by that plate with the wordmark removed, and
no other tannery could put it up, because no other tannery has these six numbers.

## Scoring

Out of the five criteria in `v2/20-direction-lab.md` §4, scored 0 to 5 each.

| | A — the plate | B — the pit book | C — the statement |

| 2. Serves the primary action | 5 | 3 | 2 |
| 3. Buildable and maintainable | 5 | 4 | 5 |

| **Total** | **25** | **19** | **14** |

### A — the plate — 25, and the winner

Criterion 1: the comp cannot exist for another firm, because it is six specific measured
substances drawn to one scale. Swap the copy and the drawing becomes wrong, not generic.
Criterion 2: the brief's job is *choose a hide, and know when*. The plate is the choosing,
before anything else on the page, and the compare control under it superimposes any two of the
six on the same baseline. Criterion 4: it is the opposite of all three anti-references — no
photograph of hands, no colour swatch, no feature grid, and nothing on it that would have to be
invented. Criterion 5: the defended thing is the plate itself, and it is the signature.

**The graft, recorded.** A takes the **dated axis with the marked line at today** from B. It is
the only part of B that A could not have arrived at on its own: B proved that the six lead
times and the two pit dates want a single shared time axis rather than a column in a table.
In A it lives in the pit section, on B's reversed ground, so that the borrowing is visible
rather than dissolved.

### B — the pit book — 19

**What it did well.** It found the shape of the "know when" half of the job before A did: one
dated axis carrying both the pit that is already down and the lead time from order, with today
marked, is the only arrangement in which the goat's eleven months stops reading as a shrug. It
also had the most authentic voice of the three, because a yard really does keep a book.

**Why it lost, specifically.** It answers the second half of the job on the first screen and
the first half two screens down. A bookbinder who has not yet chosen between a 1.2 mm goat and
a 1.4 mm calf has no use for the goat's lead time, and the ledger reads to them as six rows of
someone else's admin. The near-black ground compounds it: a millimetre comparison is read on a
bench under a window, and the substance figures are the one thing on this site that get
compared digit by digit. Putting them in pale ink on a dark field costs legibility to buy
atmosphere, on a page whose whole claim is that it is not selling atmosphere.

### C — the statement — 14

**What it did well.** The sentence is right, and it is the most quotable thing this firm owns:
*nine to fourteen months, nothing changes that*, against a chrome tannery's day. It also
handled the price problem elegantly by never getting near it.

**Why it lost, specifically.** It spends the entire first screen making a claim the visitor did
not come for, and its imagery axis is `deliberately imageless` — which for this subject means
forfeiting the only asset that makes the choice possible. `24-asset-plan.md` allows that door,
but it is the wrong door here: the visitor's job is explicitly to choose between six things
that differ by tenths of a millimetre, and a page that declares imagery is not load-bearing has
declared that the choosing is done in prose. It is also the only one of the three that could be
rebadged: set that sentence in that face on that ground for a cheesemonger or a distillery and
nothing breaks. Criterion 1 caps it at 2 for that reason alone.

The sentence survives, in A, as the heading of the pit section rather than as the page.
