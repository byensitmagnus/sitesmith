# Brief — Falkner & Vane, vegetable tannery

A working tannery in Walsall that tans hides with oak and mimosa bark in pits, over months
rather than the day a chrome tannery takes. They sell leather by the hide to saddlers,
bookbinders and two shoemakers, and they will not sell to the public.

## What the visitor came to do

**A bookbinder needs to choose a hide for a specific binding and know when it can ship.**

That is one job with two halves and both have to work:

1. **Choose.** Six leathers, and the difference between them is not colour. It is thickness in
   millimetres, temper (how much it stands up versus how much it drapes), grain, and how it
   takes a fold. A bookbinder choosing between a 1.2 mm goat and a 1.4 mm calf is making a
   decision about whether the spine will crack in twenty years. Two leathers described a screen
   apart is not a choice they can make.
2. **Know when.** Bark tanning takes 9 to 14 months and nothing changes that. Every hide on the
   site is at a stage in that process, and the stage decides the date. A visitor who does not
   understand why a hide takes a year will read the lead time as incompetence.

## What is true

Every fact below is verified and may be used. Nothing outside this list may be stated as fact.

- Founded 1878, in the same pit yard. Twenty-eight pits, of which nineteen are in use.
- Bark: English oak, sourced from a named coppice in Shropshire; mimosa, imported.
- Six leathers currently available, with measured values:

  | leather | thickness | temper | grain | typical use |
  | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
  | Oak-tanned calf | 1.4 mm | firm | fine, close | book boards, spines |
  | Oak-tanned goat | 1.2 mm | soft | pronounced, irregular | full-leather bindings |
  | Bark-tanned cowhide, shoulder | 3.2 mm | very firm | coarse | saddlery, belts |
  | Bark-tanned cowhide, belly | 2.4 mm | soft | loose, stretchy | linings, gussets |
  | Mimosa calf | 1.3 mm | medium | fine | repair work, matching |
  | Oak-tanned sheep | 0.8 mm | very soft | smooth | endpapers, doublures |

- Lead times, measured from order, as at July 2026: calf 4 weeks from stock; goat 11 months
  (in pit since August 2025); shoulder 6 weeks; belly 6 weeks; mimosa calf 3 weeks; sheep 5
  months (in pit since February 2026).
- Prices are quoted per hide on enquiry and vary by size and grade. **No price may appear on
  the site.** The tannery does not publish them and inventing one would be a fabrication.
- Minimum order: one hide. There is no trade account requirement.
- They do not sell to the public and say so plainly rather than apologetically.
- The pit yard can be visited by appointment on Thursdays.
- No certifications, no awards, no customer names. Two shoemakers and a number of binders buy
  regularly, and **none has agreed to be named**, so no customer or partner mark may appear.

## What must not happen

- No invented price, rating, delivery promise, stock figure, certification or testimonial.
- No customer logos. Nobody has lent their name.
- No claim about environmental impact that is not in the list above.

## Constraints

- One page, static HTML and CSS. No build step, no framework, no external requests.
- Must work at 375, 768 and 1440, in both colour schemes.
- The lead times are the hard part of the design, not a footnote.
