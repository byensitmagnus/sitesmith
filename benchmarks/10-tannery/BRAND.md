# BRAND.md — Falkner & Vane

Inventory only. Nothing here is chosen; the choosing happens in `DIRECTION.md`.

## 1. What exists

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | None supplied. No vector, no raster, no description of one. |
| **Colour** | No brand colour supplied. What the firm is *surrounded* by is in `EVIDENCE.md` §4 and all of it is the colour of leather, bark or liquor. |

Everything above is **unknown or absent**, not owned and not licensed.

## 2. What is fixed and what is open

> **Fixed:** the name, *Falkner & Vane*. The 1878 date and the same pit yard. The refusal to
> sell to the public, stated plainly. The absence of any price. The absence of any named
> customer. The six measured rows and the six lead times, exactly as given.
>
> **Open:** everything visual. There is no existing mark, palette, face or photograph to
> design around, and no rebrand is being imposed on anyone, because there is nothing to
> rebrand.

The useful consequence: the direction lab is unconstrained on look and heavily constrained on
*material*. There are no pictures. There are twelve measured values, six lead times, two pit
dates and a pit count. A direction that does not make something of those has nothing else.

## 4. What has to be made

Everything. Straight into `ASSET-MANIFEST.md`:

- a mark, because the page renders one and a coloured square is not a mark;
- a favicon derived from it;
- the comparison plate, because the visitor's job is to choose between six things that differ
  by tenths of a millimetre;
- the pit calendar, because the second half of the job is knowing when;
- the yard, drawn as a count, because "twenty-eight pits, nineteen in use" is the only figure
  on the page that says how big this place is.

All five are drawn. There is no fifth option here: no photography exists, no photography can
be commissioned, and generation is unavailable. `25-assets.md` puts "drawn" fourth in its
order of preference and notes it is *"often the strongest answer and it is under-used"* for
subjects whose world is not photogenic. A pit of brown liquor in a shed is not photogenic. A
hide in section, at one scale, next to five others, is the argument.
