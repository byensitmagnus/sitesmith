# Comp A — the plate

The page opens on the evidence and there is no hero at all. Six leathers are drawn edge on,
on one baseline, against rules a millimetre apart, so the two tenths of a millimetre between a
1.2 mm goat and a 1.4 mm calf is a thing you look at rather than a thing you are told. The
argument is that this firm's only real material is measured values, so the measured values are
the first screen. Everything else on the page hangs beneath the plate: the specification table
repeats it in figures, the pit calendar answers the second half of the job, and the enquiry
form takes the leather you chose.

- composition: the plate first, six hides in section on one shared baseline, starting immediately under a two-line masthead
- type: an old-style serif at plate scale over the same serif for text, figures in a tabular monospace
- colour: buff ground the colour of undyed bark-tanned leather, one russet accent
- imagery: diagram-led, drawn sections on a common baseline
- rhythm: one continuous field, sections divided by a tally rule rather than by a change of ground
