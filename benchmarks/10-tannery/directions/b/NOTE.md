# Comp B — the pit book

The yard keeps a book of what went down and when, and this comp makes that book the site. The
first screen is a dated ledger on a near-black ground: one line per leather, a hatched bar for
the time a hide has already spent in the pit, a solid bar for the lead time from order, and a
marked line at today. The argument is that the tannery's real subject is time, not leather, and
that a buyer who understands the pit book will forgive eleven months. Everything about
substance and temper arrives afterwards, as a table under the ledger.

- composition: a dated ledger of every hide and every pit, full width, on a near-black ground
- type: monospace throughout, letterspaced small capitals as the display face
- colour: near-black ground the colour of a pit looked into, one amber accent for today
- imagery: data-led, the ledger itself is the picture, no drawing beside it
- rhythm: alternating dark grounds, one band per part of the argument
