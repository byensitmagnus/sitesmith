Make it easy

What to tell us

  • The building type and roughly when it was built. “1970s concrete frame office, Bradford” tells us more than three paragraphs will.
  • What has happened, or what you need. Water coming in, lease ending, purchase under offer.
  • Your deadline. Exchange date, lease expiry, court timetable, board meeting. This is the thing most often left out and the thing that decides whether we can help.
  • Whether anyone else is involved. Another surveyor, a solicitor, a contractor who has already had a go at it.
  • The best number to call and when. We would rather ring you than start an email thread.

Please do not send confidential documents with a first enquiry. Leases, reports and correspondence are welcome once we have confirmed we can act and there is no conflict on the building.
