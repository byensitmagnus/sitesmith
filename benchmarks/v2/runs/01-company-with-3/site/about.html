§ 01How we work

Four surveyors, and the one you speak to writes the report

Around 180 instructions a year between four chartered surveyors is deliberate. It is enough work to keep everybody in the building types we are good at, and few enough that nothing gets delegated to somebody who has not seen the building.

There is no team of assistants producing drafts. The surveyor who inspects is the surveyor who writes, and the surveyor who writes is the one who will sit in the meeting when it is challenged. That is the whole operating model and it is the reason we cannot take everything.

“We are not the cheapest and we are not trying to be. Every report we write can end up in front of a court, so it has to be defensible. We would rather turn down work than write something we cannot stand behind.”

§ 04Clients

Why there are no client names on this site

We work almost entirely for commercial landlords, asset managers and managing agents, and a good deal of it is contentious. Publishing who instructed us on what is not ours to do.

We have not asked our clients for permission to name them, so we do not name them. If you want references, ask when you call and we will go and ask them properly. It takes a day or two and it is the right way round.

There are no testimonials on this site for the same reason. We would rather have nothing here than something we invented.

§ 05Limits

What we turn down

  • Residential homebuyer reports — RICS Home Survey Level 1, 2 or 3. Not occasionally, not as a favour
  • Instructions where we are already acting on the other side, or anywhere close enough to it that we should not be
  • Deadlines that do not allow the investigation the question needs
  • Work outside the building types and the region we are actually good at, where somebody else would do it better

Turning work down is not a marketing position. Four surveyors doing 180 instructions a year cannot take everything, and the alternative to saying no is a report written in a hurry by somebody who has not seen enough of that kind of building.
