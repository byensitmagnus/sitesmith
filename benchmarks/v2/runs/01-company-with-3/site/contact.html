What to tell us

Four things, and the call is usually five minutes.

  • The building — type, roughly when it was built, roughly how big, and where it is
  • What has happened — the defect, the lease event, or the purchase
  • The deadline — the date that actually matters, and what happens if it is missed
  • Who the client is — so we can check we are not already acting on the other side

    § 03Before you ring

    One thing worth checking first

    We do not do residential homebuyer reports

    Not an RICS Home Survey Level 1, 2 or 3, and not a survey on a house or a flat somebody is buying to live in. We are asked about twenty times a week and the answer has not changed since 2004. This practice works on commercial property for landlords, managing agents and their advisers.

    Commercial, mixed-use and residential investment property is a different question — ring and describe it.

    If you are not sure which of the three services you need, say what has happened and we will tell you. It is frequently a defect question wearing the clothes of a dilapidations one.
