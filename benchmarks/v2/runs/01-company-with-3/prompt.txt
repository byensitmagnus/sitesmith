# Brief 01 — multi-page company site

> Handed to the agent verbatim, in both arms. Do not add context to one and not the other.

---

Build the website for **Kestrel Survey**, an independent building surveying practice in
Leeds. Four people, twenty-two years old, works mostly for commercial landlords and
managing agents rather than homebuyers.

**What they sell.** Three services: pre-purchase building surveys for commercial property,
dilapidations advice for landlords and tenants at lease end, and defect diagnosis when
something has gone wrong and nobody agrees whose fault it is. The third is the one they are
known for and the one that brings the other two.

**Who reads the site.** Property managers and asset managers at agencies, usually looking for
someone specific after a recommendation, sometimes comparing two or three firms. They are
not price-shopping. They are checking that the firm is real, has done this kind of building
before, and will produce a report their client's solicitor will accept.

**What they want to happen.** An enquiry by email or phone with the building type and the
deadline. Not a form with fifteen fields; they want to be called back.

**What matters to them, in their words.** "We are not the cheapest and we are not trying to
be. Every report we write can end up in front of a court, so it has to be defensible. We
would rather turn down work than write something we cannot stand behind."

**Facts you may use.** Founded 2004. Four chartered surveyors, RICS regulated. Around 180
instructions a year. Offices in Leeds, work across Yorkshire and the North East. Notable
building types: mill conversions, 1960s concrete-frame offices, retail parks, listed
industrial. They do not do residential homebuyer reports and say so on the phone twenty times
a week.

**Constraints.** No stock photography of handshakes or hard hats. They have photographs of
buildings they have surveyed, which they are willing to use. No client names without
permission, and permission has not been asked for yet.

**Scope.** A complete site, not one page. At minimum: home, the three services, about the
practice, and contact. Add a page if the brief needs one and say why.

Use whatever stack you think is right. It must run as static files served from a directory.
