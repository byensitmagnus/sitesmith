What to put in it

  • The building type Age, construction and what it is used for. “1970s concrete-frame office, multi-let, seven storeys” tells us more than three paragraphs.
  • The deadline Exchange date, lease expiry, hearing date, or the meeting you need it for. If there is no deadline, say that too — it changes what we suggest.
  • What has happened One or two sentences. If there are previous reports, say so; we will want to read them, including the ones that disagree.
  • Who else is involved Landlord, tenant, contractor, insurer, solicitors. We check conflicts before discussing anything in detail.
  • A number and a good time We would rather ring you than exchange six emails.

Buying a house or a flat? We do not carry out residential homebuyer reports at any level. Ring anyway if you like and we will point you towards a residential practice — it will be quicker than searching.
