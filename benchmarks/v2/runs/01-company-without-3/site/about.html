The practice

Four surveyors, twenty-two years, one office.

Kestrel Survey was founded in Leeds in 2004 and has stayed deliberately small. Four chartered surveyors, around 180 instructions a year, and no layer between you and the person who goes to the building.

03 — How we work

“We are not the cheapest and we are not trying to be. Every report we write can end up in front of a court, so it has to be defensible. We would rather turn down work than write something we cannot stand behind.”

That sentence gets said to most new clients in the first phone call, and it is not a sales line. It has three practical consequences, and they are worth being clear about before you instruct us.

We will tell you what the evidence supports, and no more

Sometimes the answer is that the cause cannot be established without opening up, or that two mechanisms are both contributing and cannot be cleanly apportioned. That is a less satisfying report to receive. It is also the one that does not fall over when somebody competent reads it.

We turn work down

Where the scope is too narrow to reach a safe conclusion. Where the timescale would not allow a proper inspection. Where we already act for the other side. Where what is actually wanted is a particular conclusion rather than an investigation. It is a short conversation, it is better had at the beginning, and nobody has ever complained about being told early.

We are not the cheapest quote you will get

A defensible report takes longer to produce than one that is not. If price is the deciding factor on your instruction, we will say so plainly rather than cut the scope until the fee fits — a survey with the difficult parts removed is worse than no survey, because it looks like one.

04 — Professional

Regulation, conflicts and complaints.

RICS regulation
The practice is regulated by RICS and all four surveyors are chartered members. We work to the RICS Rules of Conduct and to the relevant professional statements and guidance notes for each service.
Insurance
Professional indemnity insurance to RICS minimum terms, with run-off cover. A certificate and the current limit of indemnity are provided on request and are stated in every fee proposal.
Conflicts of interest
Checked at enquiry, before the matter is discussed in any detail. We act for landlords and for tenants, but never both on the same building. If we are already engaged on the other side we will tell you that and nothing else.
Expert instructions
Where instructed as an expert witness, the duty is to the court and overrides the duty to the client paying the fee. Reports are prepared in the form required by Part 35 of the Civil Procedure Rules, with the statement of truth and the required declarations.
Complaints
We operate a complaints handling procedure, as RICS requires. Complaints should be made in writing to the practice at the address below; they are acknowledged within seven days and investigated by a partner who was not involved in the matter. If we cannot resolve it, the complaint may be referred to the independent redress scheme named in our procedure, a copy of which is available on request.
Data
Reports, photographs and correspondence are kept for fifteen years, which is the longest period over which a claim can realistically arrive. We do not publish client names or building addresses without written permission.
