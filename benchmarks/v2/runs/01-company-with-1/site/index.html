01

Why people call

What is usually happening when the phone rings

  • Something has failed, and the accounts of why do not match

    Water is getting in, the render is coming away, or a slab has moved. The contractor, the previous owner and the insurer each have a version of the cause. Until the cause is actually established, nobody can price the repair and nobody can say who pays for it.

  • A lease is ending and there is a schedule to answer

    Either you are serving one or one has landed on your desk. Every item has to be defensible on its own, the loss has to be evidenced rather than asserted, and the timetable started before anyone told you about it.

  • A purchase, and the report will be read by somebody else

    Your client’s solicitor will read it. So may their lender, and if the deal goes wrong afterwards, their counsel. A survey written to be skimmed is no use to any of them.

04

Standards

Why our reports read the way they do

  • We record what we saw, what we could not see, and what follows from both.
  • Every conclusion is tied to the evidence it rests on, and dated.
  • Where the evidence will not carry a conclusion, the report says that instead.
  • Four chartered surveyors, regulated by RICS. The person who inspected is the person who writes.

05

Fees and limits

What it costs, and what we will not take on

We quote a fixed fee once we know the building and the deadline. For defect work that is usually after a short call, because the fee depends on how much has to be opened up before anyone can be sure. There is no charge for the call.

We do not carry out residential homebuyer reports. RICS Home Survey Levels 1 to 3 are a different product for a different reader, and we will tell you so on the telephone rather than take the instruction.
