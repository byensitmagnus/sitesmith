What to tell us

  • The building. Type, approximate age and where it is. “1968 concrete-frame office in Bradford” is enough to start.
  • What has happened. The defect, the schedule that has arrived, or the purchase that is under way.
  • The deadline. The real one, including the date somebody else has already fixed.
  • Who you are acting for. Landlord, tenant, purchaser, or the managing agent for one of them.
  • A number to call back on. That is the whole enquiry.

What happens after you call

  • A ten-minute conversation about the building and the question. No charge.
  • If it is work we should take, a written scope and a fixed fee, usually the same day or the next.
  • If it is not, we say so on that first call and, where we can, point you somewhere better.
  • Once instructed, a date for the inspection and a date for the report, both of which we hold to.

02

Before you call

Two things worth knowing first

We do not carry out residential homebuyer reports. No houses, no flats, no RICS Home Survey Levels 1 to 3. If that is what you need, a residential surveyor local to the property is the right call, and it is a quicker conversation for both of us if you know that now.

We are not the cheapest. The reports are written to be defensible, which takes longer than writing them to be delivered. If price is the deciding factor, we are probably not the right practice.
