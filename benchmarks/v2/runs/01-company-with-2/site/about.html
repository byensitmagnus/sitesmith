01The practice

What a four-person practice is for

We are not a department of a national firm and we are not trying to become one. Four surveyors is enough to cover the range of work that comes through, and small enough that the person who inspected the building is the person who wrote the report and the person who takes your call about it eighteen months later.

About 180 instructions a year across four of us. That is enough to keep everybody in buildings often enough to stay useful, and few enough that nobody is signing something they have not read properly.

Most of it arrives through defect diagnosis. Somebody has an argument they cannot settle, we settle it in writing, and the dilapidations and the pre-purchase work tends to follow from there rather than the other way round.

02Standard

The sentence the practice runs on

We are not the cheapest and we are not trying to be. Every report we write can end up in front of a court, so it has to be defensible. We would rather turn down work than write something we cannot stand behind.

Kestrel Survey

That decides more about how this practice works than anything else about it. It is why the reports state what we could not establish rather than passing over it. It is why we will not act for both sides of the same building. It is why we ask about your deadline before we talk about the fee, and why we would rather decline an instruction on the first call than deliver it late or thin.

We do not do residential homebuyer reports. Not at any level, and not private house defects. This is a commercial practice. If you have arrived here looking for a house survey, the RICS Find a Surveyor service is the quickest route to somebody who does them properly.

04Regulation

Regulated by RICS, and what that gives you

In practice it means the firm holds professional indemnity insurance, operates a complaints handling procedure, and works under the RICS Rules of Conduct. The insurance certificate and the complaints procedure are available on request.

If you ever need the complaints procedure, it asks you to put the complaint in writing to the practice first. It names who will deal with it, how long that should take, and where it goes if you are not satisfied with the answer.

To be supplied by the practice

The RICS firm regulation number, the company registration number, the VAT number and the registered office address, all for the footer of every page. They are deliberately absent rather than approximated, because a wrong number here is worse than a missing one.
