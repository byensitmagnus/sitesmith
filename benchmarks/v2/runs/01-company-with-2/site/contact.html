    03Not for us

    Two things we will tell you on the call

    • Residential

      We do not do homebuyer reports at any level, and we do not do private house defects. We say this on the phone about twenty times a week, which is why it is on the front page as well as this one. The RICS Find a Surveyor service is the quickest route to somebody who does that work properly.

    • A conflict

      If we are already instructed on the other side of your building, we cannot take it. We will say so at the start of the call, before you have told us anything you would rather we did not know, and we will tell you who else in the region does this work.
