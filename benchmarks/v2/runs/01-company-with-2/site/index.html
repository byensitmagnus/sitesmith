01The problem

Nobody agrees whose fault it is

Water is coming into a second-floor office. The roofing contractor says the covering is sound and blames the windows. The window installer says the parapet was never detailed properly. Your client wants to know who pays, the tenant wants to know when it stops, and there is a lease event in nine weeks.

That is most of our work. Not a condition rating out of three — a written explanation of what is actually happening, why it is happening, and what it would take to stop it, with the evidence set out so that whoever disagrees has to argue with the evidence rather than with us.

It is the service we are known for, and it is the one that brings us the other two.

04Standard

Why the reports hold

Every report we write can end up in front of a court. That is not a boast, it is a constraint, and it decides how they are written.

  • The limits are in the report

    What we could not see, could not open up and could not establish is stated, together with what it would take to establish it. A report that does not say what it did not look at is the easiest kind to take apart.

  • The alternatives are on the record

    The causes we considered and rejected are written down with the reason. That is the part that survives being cross-examined, and it is the part most reports leave out.

  • The evidence is referenced

    Photographs numbered against a plan, readings with the instrument and the conditions named, dates on everything. So a second surveyor can go and check.

You will not find a client list, a logo wall or a testimonial anywhere on this site. We work for landlords and managing agents who have not been asked whether we may publish their names, and until they are asked, we will not. If you need a reference before instructing us, ask on the phone.
