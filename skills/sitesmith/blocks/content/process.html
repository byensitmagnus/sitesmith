What happens after you call

Four steps, with what each one costs you in time. Nothing here needs you on the roof.

  1. We look at it

    Two of us, ladder and a moisture meter, every junction photographed. You do not need to be there if we can get access.

    1 hourWithin four working days

  2. You get the finding

    A written report with the photographs and the readings, and a fixed price for the work it implies. If we think it can wait a season, the report says so.

    2 daysAfter the survey

  3. You decide, in your own time

    The price holds for sixty days. We do not follow up more than once, and the report is yours whether you use us or not.

    Up to 60 daysNo expiry pressure

  4. We do the work in one block

    Materials on site before we start, so the roof is open for a known window. You get the before-and-after photographs for your building file.

    1 to 3 daysScheduled with you
