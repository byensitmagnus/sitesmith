---
title: Buying
read: build step 4, when the visitor is buying or looking at a price
---

Each obligation names what it leaves free; free axes belong to the direction.

### The object and the way to get it arrive together

The buyer takes in the thing and the means of acquiring it at once. On a listing the
objects start on the first screen. Arrangement is free.

Three ways:
- A specification whose first row is the acquire line.
- A full-bleed photograph, commitment spoken across it.
- A configurator carrying its total from step one.

### The price is found, not hunted

Locatable without search wherever the object appears. A reduction names a figure the
object actually sold at; never invent a former price. Emphasis is free.

Three ways:
- One column of a dense listing, same position every row.
- Carried on the object's own label inside the photograph.
- Spoken in the configurator's sentence as choices change.

### One control commits, and it is always in reach

One control acquires the object and cannot be mistaken for another; reserving a colour
is one answer, not the answer. It is reachable from anywhere the buyer might decide.
The distinction is free.

Three ways:
- The only filled control among outlines.
- Alone in its band behind a rule, at any weight.
- Repeated at the close of every section.

### The whole commitment is legible before it is made

Everything the buyer pays, waits for and may return is readable at the moment of
commitment without navigating away. Stock, offer and delivery state are in words, never
colour alone, and settled before checkout. Placement is free.

Three ways:
- A panel holding price, variant, delivery cost and time.
- One sentence naming what is paid, the wait, and what may be returned.
- A confirm page restating it before anything is charged.

### Nothing moves near money

No figure animates on a price, in the basket or at checkout. Adding, filtering and
recalculating delivery each answer inside a tenth of a second, whatever the server
takes. The answer's form is free.

Three ways:
- The figure is simply different on the next paint.
- A written line states what changed while figures hold still.
- The control rewrites its own word to the completed one.

### The set narrows on the buyer's nouns

Narrowing runs on the buyer's own nouns, not the warehouse's. The count is always
known, an empty result offers the nearest thing that exists, and the buyer returns with
filters intact. What they have gathered stays reachable. The mechanism is free.

Three ways:
- Named facets over a dense result, counted above, state in the address.
- One question at a time reducing a stated remainder, each step re-enterable.
- A written index of categories with sizes, and a line of what is held.

### Imagery says what a photograph cannot

More than one angle, treated alike across the catalogue. Scale is established
explicitly. Where the real photograph does not exist, a labelled placeholder naming
subject, crop and ground is a finished answer and a generated one is not. Treatment is
free.

A brief that supplies no photograph and forbids generating one leaves exactly one route,
and it is written down here so nobody has to infer it: build the drawn answer `look.md`
section 3 describes, name the missing photograph in the manifest, and ship the run with
`--draft`. That is a complete answer to this obligation, not a way around it.

Three ways:
- Studio shots treated alike, dimensions written on each frame.
- Objects held, worn or in use, so a body carries the scale.
- Measured drawings to a stated scale, labelled where no shot exists.

### Every assertion is checkable on the same surface

Each claim resolves to a specification line here; a benefit without one is cut. Every
specification that exists is shown, grouped when many, never curated. Proof carries its
size, spread and worst cases: a mean from three is not a mean from three hundred. Where
proof is absent, say so and why. Presentation is free.

Three ways:
- A specification where each claim points at its row.
- A distribution shown whole, with its count.
- A line naming the absence and its reason.

## The audit

Every element is helping the purchase or in its way. Name which, and remove the second
list.
