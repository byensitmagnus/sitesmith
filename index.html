Visual preflight

SiteSmith v1.0 is in visual preflight.

Legacy benchmarks remain available in the repository as technical evidence, not as current showcase work.

There is no gallery here yet, and that is deliberate. A showcase should show work that someone who did not build it has looked at and judged good enough to ship. Nothing has cleared that bar, so nothing is on display.

Where the work actually is

  • The skill — skills/sitesmith, with the pipeline it runs in PIPELINE.json.
  • Pre-release status — the release gap plan, and the blind review rounds gating it: round 1, round 2.
  • The legacy v1 benchmark pages — still in benchmarks/. They document technical checks (contrast, focus, overflow, dead links, axe) and are not evidence of visual quality. The audit of them says why.
A gallery goes up when three cases have passed a blind review with their real scores published beside them, and not before.
