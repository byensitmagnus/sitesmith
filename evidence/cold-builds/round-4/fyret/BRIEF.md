# Hirtshals Fyr

A working lighthouse with a small room open to the public. Run by two keepers under the
maritime authority.

## What they need

A page for someone who has seen the light from the beach or from a boat and wants to know
what its flashes mean, how far it reaches, and whether they can go up. Nothing is for
sale on the page.

## Facts (the only facts; nothing else may be stated as true)

- Lit in 1863. The tower is 35 m and the light sits 57 m above sea level.
- Light character: Fl(3) W 30s. Three flashes of 0,3 seconds, 3 seconds apart, then 21,1
  seconds of darkness, repeating every 30 seconds.
- Nominal range 18 sea miles. The lens is a first-order Fresnel of 920 mm focal length,
  rotating on a mercury bath.
- The fog signal is one blast of 3 seconds every 60 seconds, sounded when visibility falls
  below 2 sea miles.
- 154 steps to the gallery. No lift. The gallery is closed in winds above 15 m/s.
- Open Tuesday to Sunday 10:00 to 16:00 from April to October. Closed November to March
  except by arrangement.
- Two keepers: Sonja Kvist and Aage Thomsen. Telephone 98 94 22 71.
- Address: Fyrvej 27, 9850 Hirtshals.
