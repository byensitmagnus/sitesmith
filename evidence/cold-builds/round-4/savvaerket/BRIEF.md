# Rold Savværk

Saws and kiln-dries hardwood from Rold Skov and sells it by the plank to furniture makers
and boat builders. Five people, one band saw, two kilns.

## What they need

A page a furniture maker can order planks from. They know what quarter-sawn means and
what moisture content matters. They want to know what is in stock, in what thickness, at
what moisture, and what it costs.

## Facts (the only facts; nothing else may be stated as true)

- Sawing since 1958. Five people. One 1,3 m band saw, two kilns of 22 m³ each.
- Four species in stock, priced per cubic metre of planed board:
  - Bøg, 27, 40 and 52 mm, 8.900 kr/m³.
  - Eg, 27, 40, 52 and 65 mm, 19.400 kr/m³.
  - Ask, 27 and 40 mm, 12.700 kr/m³.
  - Elm, 40 mm only, 16.200 kr/m³, and there are 3,4 m³ left of it.
- Everything is dried to 8 percent, measured at the centre of the plank with a resistance
  meter, and the reading is written on the end grain.
- A kiln cycle is 21 days for 27 mm and 34 days for 52 mm. Green stock can be reserved
  before it goes in.
- Planks are 2,4 to 4,8 m long, sold in whole planks, not cut to length.
- Collection from the yard, or a lorry to Jutland and Funen at 1.450 kr per load.
- Yard: Røverstuevej 6, 9520 Skørping. Telephone 98 39 12 40, weekdays 07 to 15.
