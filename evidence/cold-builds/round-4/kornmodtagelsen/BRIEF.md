# Kornmodtagelsen, Hobro Andel

An internal screen. One intake operator per shift weighs in grain lorries at harvest,
takes a sample from each, and directs the load to a silo. It runs on a wide screen in the
weighbridge hut, from 06:00 until the last lorry, used by someone who has done it for
fifteen harvests.

## What they need

The screen the operator works from. It must make the lorry that is waiting and where its
load can go obvious without being read, and let the operator direct that load from the
same screen.

## Facts (the only facts; nothing else may be stated as true)

- Each lorry is weighed in loaded and out empty. The difference is the load.
- A sample is drawn with a spear and read for moisture in percent, protein in percent and
  foreign matter in percent.
- Grain above 15,0 percent moisture goes to the drier, not to a silo. Foreign matter above
  2,0 percent is rejected and the lorry leaves loaded.
- Eight silos. Each holds one crop only: 1 to 4 are wheat, 5 and 6 are barley, 7 is rye,
  8 is oats. Each has a capacity in tonnes and a current fill.
- A direction names the lorry, the silo and the reading it was based on. It cannot be
  changed once the lorry has left the bridge.
- The bridge reads every second. Between lorries it reads zero.
- The shift runs 06:00 until the last lorry, and the queue is longest between 11 and 15.

## Constraints

- Danish copy. Single self-contained HTML file, no framework, no external JS. Google

- No invented thresholds and no invented farm names beyond plausible ones.
