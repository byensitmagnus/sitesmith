## Notes

**Photography would be wrong here, not merely unavailable.** This is a working screen in a
cellar, not a page about a brewery. Mode P is the mode where "deliberately imageless" is
usually the right answer, and the two drawings that do exist earn their place by carrying
information — which size of cask — that the eye reads faster than a word at four feet.

**Inlined, not `<img src>`.** The same silhouette is ink on the board and dimmed in the
history table, which needs `currentColor`.

**One treatment.** Both casks share stroke weight, hoop opacity and construction; the
kilderkin is the firkin fuller in the belly, which is what the difference actually is.
