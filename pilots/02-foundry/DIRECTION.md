# DIRECTION — Marrow & Kell

**Chosen: B, the workshop at night.** Built from empty. No palette, class name, layout or
drawing carried over from the version that failed two blind rounds.

## The signature

**The metal is lit and nothing else is.** Three photographs in the order the work happens — the
curl coming off the tool, the bell on the boring machine, the cut surface after — each one a
real surface at close range under one hard lamp, with everything unlit falling to near-black.
A foundry is heat and weight, and the way to say so is to photograph metal, not to draw it.

- signature-selector: .open img, figure img
- signature-min-share: 22

## Why the previous direction was abandoned rather than improved

One line drawing held the left half of every screen and never changed. Reviewer C, blind, put
it exactly: the page argues before-and-after and its only picture is captioned "before
re-tuning" — it never draws the after. Reviewer D called the same drawing inert. A third
reviewer named the bell glyph itself as generic.

So the drawing is gone entirely, and the before-and-after is no longer a caption problem:

**The table is a control, not a printout.** Before and After is a switch. Press it and five
partials move — the figures change, the deviation bars slide toward the centre line, and the
summary underneath changes from "the worst partial is 38 cents out, audible from the ground" to
"nothing further than 3 cents from true, and 9.4 kg of bronze gone for good". The page's whole
claim is that tuning is a measurable, irreversible alteration, and now a visitor watches it
instead of reading two columns.

## The enquiry, which was the other failure

Both rounds found the form neglected: near-invisible field borders, dim labels, and one control
that was the operating system's rather than the page's, on the page whose job is sending an
enquiry.

- The route to it is in the masthead **and** in the first screen, as a filled metal button.
- Fields have a real fill and a border that responds to hover.
- The select is the page's own, with a drawn mark.
- An invalid submission names the field, keeps everything typed, and moves focus to the first
  thing wrong. A valid one produces a success state that reads the enquiry back.

## Axis record

- composition: a full-bleed photograph with the claim set into its dark side, then even sections
  under one rule
- type: condensed grotesque capitals over a system sans, tabular figures for the cents column
- colour: near-black ground, one metal accent that is the colour of a freshly cut bronze
  surface, and a single warm red kept for validation
- imagery: photography-led, three process photographs, one lighting treatment
- rhythm: a poster, then evenly weighted sections divided by a single hairline

## What the page refuses to do

- No serif, no gold, no polished bell on black with a rim light. That is a distillery's costume
  and the brief forbids it.
- No cathedral, no golden hour, no sparks — tuning is cut on a lathe and the pictures show one.
- It publishes the unflattering figure. The before column is the reason to believe the after.
