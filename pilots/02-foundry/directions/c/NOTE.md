# Direction C — the tuning book

One card per bell in the ring, each carrying its weight, its nominal and its worst partial.
The argument is that a tower captain is not choosing a founder, they are trying to work out
which of their eight bells is the problem, and the site should answer that before it
introduces anybody.

Warm grey ground with one sea-green, from the verdigris of an old bell rather than from the
fresh metal.

- composition: a grid of many small things
- type: system sans with mono figures, few sizes
- colour: warm grey ground, one sea-green accent
- imagery: diagram-led, the mark repeated small on every card
- rhythm: a card grid over a banded footer
