A bell is tuned by taking metal away

There is no putting it back. We measure five partials before a bell goes on the machine and we publish both sets of figures, because a foundry that will not show you the before is asking you to take the after on trust.

What re-tuning actually moves

A bell has five partials that matter. Tuning is cutting metal from the inside at the heights where each one sounds — the hum near the lip, the nominal near the shoulder. Cut in the wrong place and you have moved two partials to fix one.

What we will not do

Re-tune a bell that is cracked

A crack in the soundbow moves as the bell is cut. We will weld it or we will re-cast it, and we will say which before you commit to either.

Work without a faculty

Bells in a church are under faculty jurisdiction. We can advise your architect and we can wait, but we will not lift a bell out of a frame on a verbal from a churchwarden.

Quote from a photograph

Weights and notes are on record in Dove's Guide. Everything else — the frame, the fittings, the state of the headstocks — needs somebody in the tower.

Tell us about the ring

We answer every enquiry ourselves, usually within a week. If the tower is in the middle of a faculty application, say so — it changes what we can usefully do first.
