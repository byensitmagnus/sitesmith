## The QA that produced these

Four candidates for the swarf and the bell, three for the bronze. **All three were accepted on
the first pass** — no iterations used, against a ceiling of two.

Each was judged against the factual risk its plan names:

- **`swarf`** — the risk was depicting a process the foundry does not use. There are no sparks
  and no grinding wheel; tuning is cut on a lathe and the picture shows a lathe tool.
- **`bell-on-the-machine`** — the risk was a bell hanging in a frame, which would contradict the
  foundry's stated refusal to work on a bell in situ. It is mouth-up on a machine bed.
- **`bronze-after`** — the risk was gold. Bell metal is a pale grey-gold bronze and a gold-graded
  surface would pull the page into the luxury costume the brief forbids. It reads as bronze.
