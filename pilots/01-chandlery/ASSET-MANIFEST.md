## What is deliberately not here

**No photograph for the polypropylene line.** Polypropylene is sold in the trade's safety blue
and hazard orange. The three-strand study on this page is white polyester, and putting it
against a polypropylene price and breaking load would show a buyer a different material from
the one the row sells. The line is out of stock, so the row states the construction in words
and says why there is no picture. It goes in `ASSET-REQUESTS.md` for the next coil.

**No line drawings.** The rebuilt page has none. Diagrammatic cross-sections carried the
previous version and two blind rounds found the same device on all three pilots.

## The QA that produced these

Four candidates for each of the bench and the three-strand study, three for the others, judged
in the page rather than on their own.

Two assets took an iteration, and both for a reason recorded in the plan:

- **`bench-measure`** — the first four candidates came back warm and lamp-lit, which is a second
  treatment on a page whose product studies are cool and flat. Regenerated with the light named
  explicitly. One iteration.
- **`rope-eight-plait`** — the first three candidates rendered a fine woven braid, which is a
  different construction from the one the row sells and the exact factual risk the plan names.
  Regenerated describing the square plait and the strand count. One iteration.

Nothing exceeded the two-iteration ceiling.
