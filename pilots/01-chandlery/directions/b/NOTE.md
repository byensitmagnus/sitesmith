# Direction B — the coil

One rope at a time, treated as an object. The drawing is enlarged until it stops being an
icon and becomes the thing on the page, on a dark ground so the white line reads as a
technical plate rather than an illustration. The argument is that a chandler chooses a
construction once and then buys it for years, so the page's job is to make one construction
comprehensible rather than to lay sixty-one of them side by side.

Its weakness is visible immediately: it is one product per screen in a shop that sells
sixty-one lines, and the comparison the buyer came to make is not on the page.

- composition: single object, centred, on a ground
- type: large serif display with mono figures
- colour: dark ground, no accent
- imagery: object-led, one drawing at plate scale
- rhythm: one continuous field, centred
