# DIRECTION — Trelfall & Son

**Chosen: A, the counter you can see.** Built from empty. Nothing in the previous version was
carried over — not the palette, not a class name, not the table, not the drawings.

## The signature

**The material is the argument, at the size a buyer decides at.** Every line opens with a
photograph of its own cut end on the same bench under the same light, close enough that the lay,
the braid, the sheath and the plait are four visibly different things. Choosing between them
*is* the purchase in this trade, and no amount of specification writing does what one comparable
photograph does.

- signature-selector: .shot, .bench
- signature-min-share: 12

## Why the previous direction was abandoned rather than improved

Two blind rounds, four reviewers, and the same finding from all of them: the page explained
better than it converted, the primary action was the lightest thing in its row, and two careful
reviewers concluded the shop had no length field at all. A drawn cross-section carried the page,
and the same drawn-diagram method appeared on all three pilots.

Three things follow, and they are the whole rebuild:

1. **Photographs, not diagrams.** Diagram-led was the shared studio tic. The subject is a
   physical material and it is photogenic at close range.
2. **The control is on the page.** No disclosure, no "cut a length" button that produces a
   field. Every line carries a length input, a stepper, a live price and an add control, all
   visible on load. The first one sits above the fold at 1440×900.
3. **The ticket is a counter docket, not a rail.** It does not exist before anything is cut,
   because a docket does not. It arrives along the foot of the window with the first cut,
   carries the count, the total and the batch of every length, and goes away when the last one
   is removed. A quarter of the width standing open and empty was the previous answer and it
   was wrong.

## Axis record

- composition: a wide poster, then the goods laid out two across, so two constructions are in the eye at once
- type: warm serif display over a system sans interface, with tabular figures reserved for the
  numbers that are compared down a column and used nowhere else
- colour: near-white ground so the photographs carry the colour; one oak accent taken from the
  bench in the pictures; the stamp red spent once, on the safety band
- imagery: product photography, one treatment, cool flat overcast daylight, cut ends at 62% 50%
- rhythm: a poster, a band, then a two-across field of goods with no rule between them

## What the page refuses to do

- It does not announce a range it cannot show. The caption says "every construction we cut",
  which is true, instead of "5 of 61 lines", which was a promise with no route.
- It does not put a picture of white polyester against a polypropylene price. That line has no
  photograph and says so in the row.
- It states no price, load or batch that `EVIDENCE.md` cannot point at, and the one live figure
  that is arithmetic rather than a published price carries a `data-source` saying so.
