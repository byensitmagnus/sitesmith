# Direction record

## Surface
buy

## Subject
Byens it ApS, CVR 46491661. One workshop at Ternevej 7 in Frederiksværk with a second
location in Vanløse. It builds gaming PCs and it runs IT support for companies, and both
happen at the same bench with the same hands.

## Constraints in force
Only facts published on byens-it.dk may appear. The brand colour is `#ba01c4` and the
standing instruction on this account names it: "Brand-farve = #ba01c4 (purple/lilla). IKKE
pink/magenta." Danish copy, with æøå written properly. The from-price is a campaign price
and expires.

## Assets that exist
Nothing. No photograph of the bench, no product render and no logo file was supplied for
this build. Whatever appears has to be drawn here or not appear.

## Nouns
bænk, kabinet, sidepanel, job, værksted, garanti, fortrydelsesret, erhverv, Ternevej

## The page I would have made on autopilot, and did not build
A near-black page. One neon accent, cyan or violet, glowing. A hero with a floating render
of a tower case lit from inside, angular clip-path shapes cutting across the sections,
spec cards in a three-up grid, and a headline along the lines of "Byg din drømme-PC".
Every gaming-PC shop on the internet has that page. It is also a lie about this business,
because half of what happens at Ternevej 7 is a company's IT support, which nobody
photographs under a purple light.

## Theses
1. the bench, the page is the shop's own job sheet and the two jobs are numbered on it
2. the receipt, the page leads with the terms because they are concrete and usually buried
3. the two doors, the page splits down the middle, gaming on one side and business on the other

## Case for the runner-up
For: 2. Two years of warranty and fourteen days of return are the most concrete facts
available, and most competitors bury them under a hero image. Leading with them would have
been the honest, checkable page. It lost because those terms are what every EU seller has
to offer anyway, so the page would argue on ground where this shop is identical to every
other one. It survives as JOB 03, stated plainly at the bottom.

Thesis 3 was rejected outright. It performs the split as separation when the whole argument
is that it is one bench, and it would have made the strongest claim look like an org chart.

## Built
Built: 1, axis: worksheet over showroom, because the thing a competitor cannot copy by
buying the same parts is that one bench does both jobs.

## Colour
- ground: `#e4e8ea`, the blue-grey of an antistatic mat, and `#0d1519` for the same bench with the light off
- steel: `#6b7280`, the panel of a case
- signal: `#8a0091`, the brand purple darkened until it clears AA on the light ground

A workshop, not a boutique. The saturated colour never fills anything. It marks the job
number, the active step and the one button that acts.

banned-palette-pinned-by-brief: not used, and recorded here so nobody adds it later. The
first draft grounded on `#eceee7`, which the gate correctly named as sitting in
taste-skill's premium-consumer band, and the ground was moved onto a material this
workshop actually has rather than waived.

purple-pinned-by-brief: the brand colour is `#ba01c4`, and the standing instruction on this
account is "Brand-farve = #ba01c4 (purple/lilla). IKKE pink/magenta." It is the signal
colour only, darkened to `#8a0091` on the light ground and lightened in dark mode, so it
clears contrast without becoming a different brand.

## Type
- display: "ui-sans-serif", the system face, because a job sheet is not typeset
- body: "ui-sans-serif", the same face, since the page is one document rather than a layout
- machine: "ui-monospace", every number, code and address, because that is how the shop writes them down

## Density, motion and boldness
Dense at the top bar, open in the body. One moving thing: the scroll timeline, which turns
the case and lifts its side panel off. Nothing else animates.

## Structure
A fixed job rail on the left at desktop width, one measured column of jobs beside it. No
cards anywhere.

## First screen
The address, then the sentence that says the same bench does both jobs. The drawing comes
after the claim, not before it.

## Imagery treatment
There are no photographs. The one drawing is schematic and the visible caption says so,
because it is not measured from a specific machine.

## Argument order
What we build, then what we run for companies, then what is written on the paper.

## Signature
the job number (`.jobno`) and the rail it belongs to. Its kind is a worksheet marking, not
a decoration, and it earns its place by being the scroll timeline made visible instead of
hidden.

## Risk
A page that looks like a document can read as unfinished to a buyer who expects a gaming
storefront. The mitigation is that the drawing and the signal colour carry the category,
and the risk is accepted rather than designed away.

## Answer to the risk
The risk above is answered by `.stage`, the six-face case drawn on JOB 01. It is the one thing on the page that says gaming rather than paperwork, and without it a reader expecting a storefront has nothing to hold on to.

## The shell
Who: `Byens it ApS` in the top bar with the CVR beside it. Where: `Ternevej 7, 3300 Frederiksvaerk` in the same bar, and Vanloese named next to it. Do: `Se gaming-computere` and `IT-support til erhverv`, the two buttons that leave for the shop, plus the phone number as a tel: link for anyone who would rather ring.

## Assumptions
Assumed the campaign price is current on the day this is read. It is not, for long. Assumed
the Vanløse location is a place a customer may visit, which the site states without giving
an address for it.

## Originality pass
Ran the plan against a bicycle workshop in the same town, and then against an accountant.
The bench held for the bicycle shop, which says the idea is about workshops rather than
about this one, so the job numbering was rebuilt from what this shop actually writes on a
job: a number, a category, a status. The accountant swap killed it outright, which is the
answer that mattered.
