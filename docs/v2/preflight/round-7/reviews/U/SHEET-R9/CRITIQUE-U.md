## What the sheet is doing well, named

The hero is the best single asset across anything on this sheet: a macro of swarf curling off the soundbow with the boring tool still in frame, captioned in mono — "SWARF OFF THE SOUNDBOW. EVERY CURL IS A PARTIAL MOVING." That caption is the page's whole argument in eight words and it is set as a caption, not as a headline.

The accent is genuinely rationed. Amber appears on the ENQUIRE chip in the header, the hero's primary button, the figure "23 lb", and SEND THE ENQUIRY. It does not appear on the partials bars, which are a warm grey — resisting the obvious move to colour the data is the best colour decision on the page.

The tuning section is a real invention rather than a chart pulled off a shelf: five named partials, each with the height it is cut at, plotted in cents either side of a "true" gridline, with −40 / true / +40 in mono, proper minus glyphs on the negative values, and a 1904 / 2019 book toggle. Nothing about it is transferable to another trade.

The content model is specific in ways the design honours: a "THREE THINGS WE WILL NOT DO" section placed second on the page, a faculty-jurisdiction question in the enquiry form, and a note beside the submit button saying they will refuse to quote if the job is not a tuning job.

Type is a two-voice system plus mono, and the mono is disciplined — it carries eyebrows, captions, axis labels and the cut-height sub-labels, never prose. Scored at 8 rather than 9 because the display face and the body face sit close enough that the 01/02/03 band reads as one weight ramp rather than two voices, and because the mark — a hairline bell-wall section with the cut heights ticked off it, a good idea — is drawn so light it nearly vanishes beside the wordmark at 1280.

Colour scored 8 because the ground is one flat near-black from header to footer; a hairline is the only thing separating the essay from the conversion screen, so the ground carries less of the structure than the type and the imagery do.
