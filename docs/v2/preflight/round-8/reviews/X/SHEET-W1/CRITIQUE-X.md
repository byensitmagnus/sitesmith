notes:
- colour — mobile, screens 5 and 6 (the nine drawers): each crop ends in a solid full-width red bar, so four identical red slabs stack in one 390-wide viewport and outweigh the crop names, the packet counts and the seed drawings that are the actual content; the same button on desktop is a small right-aligned block and reads correctly there.
- hierarchy — mobile, screens 5–6: squinting, the first things seen are the red bars, then the counts, then the names — the reverse of the order the choice needs; desktop screens 2–3 get this right.
- production-readiness — I would ship the desktop tomorrow without a word; on mobile I would want the button weight and the count of red blocks reduced before this went out under the library's name.
- type — the display serif and the mono labels are chosen and used hard, and the italic serif cultivar names ('Pentland Brig', 'Cheltenham Green Top') are a real recurring detail; the body face is a neutral humanist sans that carries most of the words on the page and is the least considered of the three.
- direction — "Take up to six packets of seed. In autumn, bring some back." The page states the whole bargain in the headline and then, as it says itself, spends its length explaining the return instead of enforcing it. Legible in the first screen.
- specificity — a nine-drawer lending library inside a named branch library, 228 packets counted as at a date, no budget to restock, 96 of 412 members returned seed, and two named allotment associations. Not transferable to another organisation.
- assets — one notation runs the whole page: solid silhouette for seed that comes back true, broken outline for seed that will not, declared once under "How to read the drawings" and then used in the masthead mark, the five-and-four comparison row, and every drawer entry. The pollen pair explains crossing in two drawings rather than a paragraph. The season rule with the red arc turns the promise into a shape. The 412-mark matrix prints an unflattering 96 next to a sentence refusing to round it. Borrowed proof is refused and the refusal is argued: no badges, "they lent their names, and a name belongs in type".
- assets — small deduction: on desktop screen 4 the season rule runs on past the AUTUMN tick to an unlabelled seed at its right end with no tick under it, so the terminus the red arc springs from has to be inferred; the five "comes back true" silhouettes also vary widely in optical weight, so the tomato reads as a blot beside the lettuce sliver.
