notes:
- colour — mobile screens 5 and 6: nine full-bleed brick-red buttons in the drawer list mean the accent occupies more area than any other element on the page, so the two places the red is actually carrying an argument (the return arc under "What you are promising", and the 96 filled marks in the 412 grid) lose the weight they were given.
- hierarchy — desktop screen 3: the "Your slip" card is a static panel at the top of the right rail, so once it scrolls up the drawer list runs for two-thirds of the viewport against a completely empty right third with nothing holding that edge; the slip is also the only thing that ever occupies that column.
- Against those two, the substance is strong. The first screen states the whole transaction in the H1 and puts the four facts a first-time saver needs — cost, limit, opening hours, what is actually in the drawers — in a hairline table beside it, and then admits in the standfirst that the rest of the page exists to explain the return rather than enforce it. That is a direction stated and then kept.
- Criterion 5 is where this page earns its score. The nine seed drawings are one treatment at one scale, split into five solid and four broken-outline, and the outline convention is the argument: solid means the seed you save grows the plant you took it from, broken means it will not. You can read which crops to take before reading a word, and the page teaches the convention immediately after showing it. The two pollen drawings answer why in one frame each — pollen that stays put versus pollen that travelled — and the 412-mark grid with 96 filled answers how many members actually return seed, printed rather than rounded up, with the count stated. The seasonal loop puts spring, summer and autumn on one rule with a red arc closing back to the drawer the seed came out of. Every one of those finishes the sentence "the visitor learns X because it is there".
- Borrowed proof is the best-handled of anything on these sheets: two allotment associations named in type, with an explicit note that neither has a badge because neither was asked for one, followed by a "Nothing else to declare" column stating there are no certifications, awards, funders or sponsors. Nothing is the generated default — no photograph, no gradient, no icon grid, no floating screenshot.
- Type is 8 because the crop line is genuinely distinctive (roman genus, italic quoted cultivar, mono packet count right-aligned) but the humanist sans carrying the body is neutral enough that the long explanatory middle of the page has less voice than the headings promise.
