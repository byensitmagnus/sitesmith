notes:
- production-readiness — mobile screen 4: the Specification block clips at roughly 30 px inside its own panel edge, cutting body prose mid-word ("thicknes", "smootl", "pronou / irregul", "fine, cl", "loose, s") and hiding TYPICAL USE and LEAD TIME with no scroll affordance in the still; prose should not be inside the table's overflow container at all, and I could not put this in front of a bookbinder tomorrow without saying so first.
- Everything else here is unusually good. "Six leathers, drawn to scale" is not a slogan, it is a description of what the page then does: six hide sections drawn edge on, all standing on one baseline against rules a millimetre apart, thinnest first. Each drawing carries three arguments at once and the page names them — strip height is measured thickness at that scale, the droop past the bench edge is temper turned into an ordering, and the notch rhythm cut into the top edge is grain. One treatment, one crop logic, one ink, and the focal point (strip plus figure plus temper word) survives at 390 as well as at 1280.
- The comparison is made rather than mentioned. The lay/over control puts any two of the six on one baseline and draws the thinner as a dashed outline on the thicker, which is exactly the 1.2 mm goat against the 1.4 mm calf decision the copy says binders actually face. The lead-time timeline then does the same job for the second half of the brief: quoted weeks drawn from today, with the two hides already in pits shown with their pit dates and the nine-to-fourteen-month window that follows each, and a legend that distinguishes all four marks.
- Colour is derived, not rotated: parchment ground, bark-dark ink, one oxblood that appears on the primary CTA and the active nav underline and essentially nowhere else. The single ground inversion to near-black is spent on the pits section, where the argument goes underground, and the pit grid there is honestly captioned as a count and not a survey of the yard.
- Type is 8 rather than 9 because the label layer is doing all the differentiating. The mono eyebrows, units and figures are handled with real care, but the serif body is set at one size and one colour in nearly every section, so the three-up explainers and the "How a hide is bought" blocks read as the same texture and the middle of the page flattens.
- Borrowed proof is handled correctly by refusing it: the page states that no customer has agreed to be named, carries no badge, and closes by saying nothing on it came from a camera. That earns the drawn assets rather than excusing them. Minor mobile nit: the primary nav wraps with BUYING orphaned on a second line separated by a gap larger than its own line-height, which reads as a break rather than a two-line nav.
