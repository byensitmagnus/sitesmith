notes:
- specificity — The vocabulary is unmistakable (gyle numbers, firkins and kilderkins, ullage in pints, "dray in at 07:15", "a duty entry and not just a count"), but the shell is not: take the words off desktop screen 1 and what remains — slate cards, coloured left bars, filled status pills, a three-tile count strip — is a stock dark admin theme that would carry any queue at all.
- colour — The system is taught properly by the header (red = late, amber = due today, blue = later, green = booked) and then abandoned at the one moment it matters: the refused book-in on desktop panel 3 is entirely monochrome, a white ring on the select and a white bar beside white text, so the block reads as a hint on a page that has already trained the eye that colour means "this needs you".
- assets — There is effectively no imagery: one line-drawn cask glyph repeated per row, and a header photograph so faint behind "Cask desk" that it registers as noise rather than a picture — too weak to be a photograph, too textured to be a flat ground. Spacing and alignment, by contrast, are consistent and clean at both widths.
- hierarchy — Squint at desktop screen 1 and the order is red pill, amber pill, amber pill, then four large numerals; the "Book in" buttons do not appear at all, so the page's read is triage-only and the entry step has to be hunted for. On mobile screen 1 it is worse — "Book in" is a full-width faint slate box directly under a faint slate input, and the two are indistinguishable.
- direction — The argument is real but it is carried by the ordering rule rather than by anything visual: "Late first, then due today", one row per consignment, the record made in the row it belongs to. As a surface it is the category default for an operations desk, competently done — dark slate ground, coloured severity bars, a count strip top right.
- type — One sans in three weights, with a monospace held back for record identifiers (gyle 212, ullage 11 pt) — that reservation is the one move I would recognise again. Against it, the mobile header sets "Cask desk" and "Cellar, Thursday · 06:40 · dray in at 07:15" on the same line at nearly the same size, so the title does not lead, and about 120px of empty header sits between it and the count strip.
- production-readiness — The most finished of the three at both widths: no collisions, no stuck states, a genuine empty state ("Nothing booked in yet this week…"), an Undo on the completed record, and a mobile layout that is reworked rather than squashed. The button weight is the single thing I would have to flag before it went to a cellarman.
