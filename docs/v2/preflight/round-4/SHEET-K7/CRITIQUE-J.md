- assets — Three separate seam failures: desktop screen 3, the footer rule runs along the bottom edge of the SEND THE ENQUIRY button; desktop screen 2, the "TOWER" field label sits immediately under the section rule with the photo caption "INSIDE THE SOUNDBOW, AFTER CUTTING" 15px above it, and that caption is inset about 24px right of the field column beneath it, so image and form do not share a left edge; mobile screen 1, "WHAT RE-TUNING ACTUALLY MOVES" starts roughly 10px below the hero photo's hard bottom edge.
- hierarchy — Desktop screen 3: the enquiry section leaves a void of about 450x600px under the four-line intro in the left column while the form runs alone down the right, which is the largest empty area on the page and lands on the one section the visitor came to use.
- production-readiness — Mobile screen 1: the hero paragraph runs across the bright bell-lip highlight, and "…because a foundry that will not / show you the before…" loses most of its contrast where the off-white text crosses the pale tan streak; there is no scrim strong enough at that point.
- direction — Stated in the first screen and then proved: "A BELL IS TUNED BY TAKING METAL AWAY", and two sections later a BEFORE/AFTER toggle that actually swaps the five partials from −38/+21/−14/+9/0 to −2/+1/0/+3/0 with the metal-off weights beside them. The visual argument is a foundry publishing its own before, not a foundry advertising.
- specificity — The cents-from-equal-temperament table with a zero-centred bar per partial and a kg column is the irreplaceable part; so are the "FACULTY POSITION" field and the "WHAT WE WILL NOT DO" trio. Stripped of copy, the skeleton (hero / toggle-table / three-up / split / form) is a shape another heritage-industrial brand could wear — the table is what stops it.
- type — Condensed grotesque in caps, set tight and ragged over four and five lines, doing all the shouting; humanist sans for body; letterspaced caps for captions and field labels; mono figures in the table. Recognisable on a second page. One unresolved detail: the tracking in the table pushes a wide gap between value and unit, so it reads "4.1  kg".
- colour — Near-black warm ground with brass as the only accent, and the accent carries meaning rather than decoration: the CTA, the active toggle, and — quietly — the partials that read exactly 0, which are gold while the off ones stay white. The photographs are lit like the ground, so page and image behave as one material.
