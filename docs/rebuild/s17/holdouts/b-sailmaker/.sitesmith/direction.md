# Direction record

## Surface
buy

## Subject
Grubbe Segl in Marstal, a sailmaker selling one thing: a storm jib cut to the measured length of the customer's forestay.

## Constraints in force
Only the sailmaker's own order sheet may appear. Prices in DKK excluding VAT. Danish copy with the Danish letters.

## Assets that exist
Nothing. No photograph of the sail, no loft, no boat.

## Nouns
forstag, dug, kovs, lig, rev, opmaaling, ordreseddel

## The page I would have made on autopilot, and did not build
A hero photograph of a yacht heeled over in spray, a price in large type, three trust badges, a review carousel, and an add to cart button. It sells a mood, and this product cannot be bought from a mood: it is cut to a measurement nobody has taken yet.

## Theses
1. the order sheet, the page is the document the loft fills in, and the reader fills it in with them
2. the cloth, the page leads with the material and the specification does the selling
3. the storm, the page argues from the conditions the sail exists for

## Case for the runner-up
For: 2

The cloth is where the money goes and a specification table is honest in a way a photograph is not. It lost by a small margin, and it lost on sequence rather than on content: the table is still the first thing in the left column, but the sum has to be visible while it is read, and leading with cloth pushes the sum below the fold.

Thesis 3 was rejected: arguing from the storm is arguing from fear, and a sailmaker who does that is selling insurance.

## Built
Built: 1, axis: document over storefront, because the thing being bought does not exist yet and cannot be photographed, so the honest object is the sheet that starts it

## Colour
- ground: `#e8eef1`, the grey blue of sailcloth under a workshop light, and `#0b1519` for the same cloth at night
- ink: `#0f2430`, the blue black of a chandler's stamp
- mark: `#0b6b70`, the one colour that acts, on the button and the checkbox and nothing else

## Type
- display: "Helvetica Neue", the face a form is set in, because this page is a form
- body: "Helvetica Neue", the same face, since a sheet does not change voice halfway
- machine: "ui-monospace", every price, dimension and order number

## Density, motion and boldness
Dense in the left column, still everywhere. Nothing animates. The only movement is the sum changing as a box is ticked, which is information rather than motion.

## Structure
Two columns above 900px: measurements and choices on the left, the sum sticky on the right. One column below that, sum last.

## First screen
The loft name and the order number, then the sentence that says it is cut to the measured forestay and not to the boat model.

## Imagery treatment
There are no photographs. The specification table is the illustration.

## Argument order
What it is made of, then what you can choose, then what it costs and how long it takes.

## Signature
the sticky sum panel with the two pixel rule above the total (`.sum`). Its kind is a till receipt, not a pricing card, and it is the only bordered box on the page.

## Risk
A page with no image of the product asks a lot of a buyer who has not met this loft. Accepted, and mitigated by the specification table doing the work a photograph would.

## Assumptions
Assumed the prices are current and exclude VAT, which the sheet states. Assumed the 8 to 11 week lead time is the high season figure.

## Originality pass
Swapped the subject for a bespoke bootmaker: the order sheet held, which said the idea was about made to measure rather than about sails, so the option list was rebuilt from what only a sail has, the measurement visit. Swapped it for a bookshop and it collapsed.

## One-offs
- `380px` the sum column is set to the width of the longest price line, not to a grid step
- `52px` the commit button is taller than the floor because it is pressed with a thumb on a boat
- `6px 6px 0` the sum panel casts a hard offset shadow, because a receipt lies on a sheet rather than floating above it

## Deliberate
- `none` this build claims no antipattern on purpose
