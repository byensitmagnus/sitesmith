# Direction record

## Surface
experience

## Subject
Klokkestoeberiet Hjelm, a bell foundry holding one open day. There is a single pour, at a single minute, and the rest of the day is arranged around it.

## Constraints in force
Only the foundry's own day programme may appear. Danish copy with the Danish letters. The brief asks to be carried through the day, so motion.md is in force.

## Assets that exist
Nothing. No photograph of the foundry, no recording of the bell.

## Nouns
stoebning, form, kerne, ovn, overtone, kvart tone, sand

## The page I would have made on autopilot, and did not build
A dark page with a glowing molten pour behind a centred headline, three cards for craft, tradition and quality, a countdown timer, and a parallax hero that moves faster than the text. Every craft open day page on the internet, and it hides the only thing that matters, which is that the pour happens at 11.14 and does not wait.

## Theses
1. the score, the day is a single vertical line of times and the page is read down it
2. the section, the page is a cutaway of the mould and the copy hangs off its parts
3. the recording, the page leads with the sound and the programme is secondary

## Case for the runner-up
For: 2

A bell is a shape before it is a sound, and a cutaway would have explained the three day cooling better than any sentence. It lost because the open day is a schedule, not an object: a visitor who arrives at noon has missed the thing, and the page has to say that before it says anything else.

Thesis 3 was rejected: there is no recording, and building a page around an asset that does not exist is how a placeholder ships.

## Built
Built: 1, axis: schedule over showcase, because the pour is at a minute and does not wait, and the page fails if a reader does not know that within a second

## Colour
- ground: `#cdd2c6`, the grey green of iron dust settled into the floor sand, and `#0a1410` for the same floor at night
- form: `#7d8672`, the loam of the mould once it has taken metal
- glow: `#a83308`, the metal, used only at the minute of the pour and in the overtone bars

## Type
- display: "Iowan Old Style", the serif a church list is set in, because that is where these bells end up
- body: "Iowan Old Style", the same face, since the page is one document
- machine: "ui-monospace", every time of day, because a time is a number and is read as one

## Density, motion and boldness
Open and vertical. One moving thing: seven overtone bars that fill as the page scrolls, each a pure function of the scroll number.

## Structure
One column. A single vertical rule with the times hung on it, then the overtone row, then the practical facts.

## First screen
The name, then the sentence that says the mould opens at 11.14 and does not wait.

## Imagery treatment
There are no photographs. One drawing: a section through the bell with the sound bow marked, because the wall thickness there is what produces the row above it. Schematic, and the caption says so.

## Argument order
What happens and when, then what you are actually hearing, then what to wear.

## Signature
the vertical rule with dots on it (`.partitur`). Its kind is a score, not a timeline widget, and the pour dot is the only one that is the glow colour.

## Risk
A page with no picture of a bell may read as unfinished to someone expecting a craft site. Accepted: the foundry supplied no photograph and inventing one is the failure this package exists to prevent.

## Answer to the risk
The risk above is answered by nothing, and that is why this page was rejected. The record said it may read as unfinished and the build shipped anyway. Recorded here as a failure rather than repaired, because this page is the evidence.

## The shell
There is none. One anchor on the whole page and it is the skip link. Kept visible as the defect it is.

## Assumptions
Assumed the 14 September date is the current one. Assumed the pour minute is exact rather than approximate, because the programme prints it to the minute.

## Originality pass
Swapped the subject for a glassworks open day: the score held, which said the idea was about scheduled irreversible events rather than about bells, so the overtone row was rebuilt from what only a bell has. Swapped it for a museum with opening hours and it collapsed, which is the answer that mattered.

## One-offs
- `2px` the score rule is a rule and a rule is not a scale step
- `8px` the dots on the score are sized to the rule, not to the type scale

## Deliberate
- `none` this build claims no antipattern on purpose
