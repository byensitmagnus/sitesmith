# Comp B — Snittet

The instrument opened up. The page begins with the parts drawing, framed as a plate, and the
words come underneath it as a caption to the machine. Dark ground, chalk lines, brass numerals
— the inside of the case rather than the paper that runs through it. It argues that the
customer's real question is mechanical and should be answered with a mechanism.

- direction-version: 2.3
- composition: full-bleed plate first — the framed technical section occupies the top of the page and the headline sits below it as its caption
- type: Archivo condensed-grotesque display in capitals over a Newsreader serif body, figures in the display face
- colour: near-black ground, one brass accent reserved for numerals and the action
- imagery: diagram-led, chalk line on dark, plates boxed in a heavy frame
- rhythm: alternating bands — dark field, banded strip, dark field, each separated by a rule

- surface: heavy frames — a workshop drawing is a plate with a border and a title block, and the border is what says "this is a drawing, not a picture"
- labels: display-face capitals with wide tracking — the title-block convention of the parts drawing the page is imitating
- figures: tabular motif — the numerals are the brass callouts on the drawing and the prices repeat them at size, so figures are the ornament of this comp on purpose
- depth: flat — a section drawing has no shadow in it, and adding one would make the plate look like a card

- visual-density: 4
- motion-intensity: 2
- aesthetic-boldness: 7
