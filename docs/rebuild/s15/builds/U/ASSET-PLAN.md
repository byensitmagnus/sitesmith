## `mark-klinke`

- kind: brand
- carries: that a named workshop with two people in it stands behind the page, and that its
  material is punched paper
- job: know within a second whose page this is before deciding whether to read on
- use: wordmark set in the display face at 21–26px in the masthead and again at 19px in the
  footer, with three punched slots cut through the rule beneath it
- comparative: no
- without-it: the page opens with an unattributed headline and reads as a category page about
  player pianos rather than the page of one workshop in Odense
- evidence: none — brand asset, drawn for this project

## `roll-rail`

- kind: brand
- carries: that the perforated paper is the same object twice — the thing the instrument reads
  and the thing this workshop cuts and sells
- job: recognise, on returning to the page or seeing it beside a competitor's, that this is the
  workshop that makes rolls
- use: a 116px column of buff paper with punched slots running the full height of the page to
  the left of the text column at 1000px and above; below that, a 26px horizontal punched strip
  across the top of the sheet, at the same slot rhythm
- comparative: no
- without-it: the page has a serif headline on a warm ground and nothing that could not be
  rebadged for any other repair trade; this is the single element that fails criterion 1 of the
  direction lab if it is removed
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md §1 "A music roll: perforated paper, wound on a spool" and §6 reference 1

## `snit-pneumatik`

- kind: editorial
- carries: where each of the five named failures physically sits in the chain from the pedals
  to the hammer, so that five unfamiliar words become five places
- job: work out whether the instrument in the living room is likely to be one of these cases,
  and have something to say on the phone
- use: inline SVG line drawing, one column wide, maximum 380px, sitting immediately left of the
  five disclosures at 900px and above and directly above them below that; the numerals 1–5 on
  the drawing are the same numerals as the disclosure headings
- comparative: yes — all five failure sites are in one frame, because the visitor is holding
  five candidate causes against each other and cannot do that if they are five screens apart
- without-it: the five failure names are a vocabulary list, and the section that is supposed to
  make the mechanism doubtable instead asks to be taken on faith
- evidence: the sealed brief, "Common failures they name: perished pouch leather, split tracker
  bar tubing, worn striker pneumatics, a cracked wind trunk, and drive belts that have gone
  hard", plus EVIDENCE.md §1 for the parts each name refers to

## `favicon`

## The load-bearing rule

`snit-pneumatik` is load-bearing for the visitor's primary job. The primary job is deciding
whether to spend 1.850 kr. finding out; the thing that moves that decision is recognising that
the failure list is five ordinary mechanical parts and not an unknowable interior. That
recognition is what the drawing delivers and no paragraph does.

`DIRECTION.md` declares `imagery: diagram-led`, which this plan delivers. It does not claim to
be photography-led, and there is no photograph on the page to contradict it.

## What was cut, and why

- **A drawn portrait of Verner and Liv.** `carries:` could not be written without inventing
  what they look like. Cut.
- **A drawn roll box with a title on it.** Would have required naming one of the 38 titles,
  which the brief does not give. Cut.
- **A texture over the hero.** `carries:` would have been "it makes the page look less empty",
  which is the test in `24-asset-plan.md`. Cut.
