# Klinke & Datter

Repair and re-tune player pianos and pianolas. Two people, father and daughter, workshop
in Odense. They also sell perforated music rolls they cut themselves.

## What they need

One page. A person who has inherited a player piano and does not know whether it can be
saved should understand what these people do, roughly what it costs to find out, and how
to start.

## Facts (the only facts; nothing else may be stated as true)

- Two people: Verner and Liv Klinke. Verner has done this since 1981, Liv since 2011.
- They work on pneumatic player pianos and pianolas only. Not electronic instruments and
  not ordinary pianos, though they will tune an ordinary piano if they are already there.
- A first assessment costs 1.850 kr. and takes half a day in the customer's home. It ends
  in a written condition report whether or not the job goes ahead.
- A full pneumatic restoration takes four to nine months and is quoted after the
  assessment. They will not quote before.
- Common failures they name: perished pouch leather, split tracker bar tubing, worn
  striker pneumatics, a cracked wind trunk, and drive belts that have gone hard.
- They cut new music rolls from original masters. 38 titles in stock. 340 kr. per roll.
- They take four instruments at a time and there is usually a wait.
- Phone 66 12 47 09. Workshop open Tuesday to Thursday, 09:00 to 16:00.
- Address: Havnegade 22, 5000 Odense C.
