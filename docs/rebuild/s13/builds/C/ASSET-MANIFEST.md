## Not assets, and why they are not listed

- The mottled crystalline ground of the whole page is cast lead and tin, and it is six
  `radial-gradient` layers plus one `repeating-linear-gradient` in CSS. No image file.
- The grain of the tawed skin on the leather panels is two `repeating-linear-gradient`
  layers and two radial ones in CSS. No image file.
- The hand-cut edge of every leather panel is a `clip-path` polygon. No image file.
- No icons, no illustration set, no logo mark. The name is set in type.
- No photography of any kind, because none exists and none was invented.
