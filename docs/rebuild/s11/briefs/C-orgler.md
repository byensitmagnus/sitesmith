# Orgelværkstedet Hvidbjerg

Two people who restore and voice church organs. They work on four or five instruments a
year, each job lasting months.

## Facts (the only facts; nothing else may be stated as true)

- Two people: Aase and Thorbjørn. Working together since 1998.
- Four or five instruments a year. A full restoration runs eight to eighteen months.
- Work covered: releathering bellows and pneumatics, repairing wind chests, replacing
  pipe ranks, voicing and tuning, and rebuilding tracker actions.
- They do not install electronic organs and will say so.
- A first visit is free and takes half a day. They write a condition report afterwards
  whether or not the job goes ahead.
- Based in Hvidbjerg, Thy. They travel anywhere in Denmark.
