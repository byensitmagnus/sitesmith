# Brief

**Vægtkontrol Nord** recalibrates and verifies industrial weighing equipment. Bakeries,
fishmongers, small food producers, a few pharmacies. Based in Aalborg, covering North
Jutland. Two technicians and a van.

## What they need

A one-page site. A new customer, usually a shop owner who has just been told by an
inspector that their scale is out of verification, should be able to understand what
this company does and book a visit within a minute.

- Founded 2009. Two technicians: Kirsten and Bo.
- Accredited under DANAK for non-automatic weighing instruments, classes III and IIII.
- Callout across North Jutland, typically within three working days. Emergency callout
  within 24 hours for an extra fee.
- Fixed price per instrument, quoted before the visit. No hourly billing.
- Every job ends with a calibration certificate and a verification seal on the
