Work Terms

Six pits, ninety days, no shortcut

We tan with bark liquor in open pits. A hide enters at pit one and leaves at pit six, and the ledger records the day it moved.

Pit 1, and the liquor it holds

The liquor in this pit is measured at the pit side every morning and the reading is written in the ledger before any hide is moved into it, because a reading taken afterwards is a reading of the wrong pit.

A hide entering here has already passed the pit before it, and it leaves for the next one when the ledger says it may, never before and never on anyone's memory of what the ledger said.
