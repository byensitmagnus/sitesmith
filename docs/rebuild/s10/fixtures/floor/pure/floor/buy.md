# Buying

### The price is found, not hunted
On every surface the object appears on, the price is locatable without searching for it.

Three ways this has been satisfied:
1. Stamped as a mark on the product image itself.
2. In a fixed column position in a table, identical on every row.
3. Spoken in a configurator's running sentence as choices are made.

### One control acquires the object
Exactly one control on the surface commits the buyer, and it cannot be confused with any
other control. Reserving a colour for it is one answer, not the answer.

Three ways this has been satisfied:
1. It is the only filled control among outlines.
2. It is the only control inside the purchase panel; everything else is a text link.
3. It sits alone in its band, separated by a rule, at any weight.

### The whole commitment is legible before it is made
Everything the buyer will pay and wait for is readable at the moment of commitment,
without navigating away.

Three ways this has been satisfied:
1. One panel holding price, variant, delivery cost and time beside the control.
2. A running summary that restates the total as choices change.
3. A confirm step that restates the commitment on its own page before anything is charged.
