# Buying
The price is legible before the commitment. The primary action is the most prominent
control on the surface. Touch targets are at least 44px with 24px between them.

### Three ways this has been satisfied
1. A dense tabular listing where the price sits in the same column on every row.
2. A single full-bleed object with one price set larger than the heading above it.
3. A stepped configurator where the running total is pinned to the edge of the viewport.
