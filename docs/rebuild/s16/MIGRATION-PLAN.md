# One skill, not two

## The end state, in one paragraph

`skills/sitesmith/` contains what is today `skills/sitesmith-v3/`: 24 files, 320 KB, an always-loaded surface of about 3,000 estimated tokens, plus the carries this plan names. `skills/sitesmith-v3/` stops existing as a path. The 472-file, 37 MB v2 tree leaves the shipped package entirely: its prose moves to `docs/v2/`, its two lab instruments move to `tools/`, its licence text and its dependency pin move into the surviving tree, and everything else is deleted with a written reason. `bin/sitesmith.mjs` is the one installer and it installs the one tree; `tools/install-sitesmith.mjs` is deleted after four of its behaviours move into `bin/`. `.claude-plugin/marketplace.json` keeps `"source": "./"` and stops discovering two `SKILL.md` files that both declare `name: sitesmith`, because there is only one. The always ceiling of 3,100 does not move and the shipped number falls; three scenario ceilings move once each, and each move is recorded against what it bought.
