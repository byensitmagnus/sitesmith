# Halgarth Rotor Balancing

A fictional two-person workshop that regrinds and rebalances industrial fan impellers.
They take the rotor off site, grind worn blade tips back to a true profile, and rebalance
on a horizontal soft-bearing machine. They do not sell fans, do not fit fans, and do not
go to site.

## Who it is for

Maintenance engineers with a fan that is shaking and a production line that cares.

## What is true

Maximum rotor mass 900 kg. Maximum diameter 2,200 mm. Standard balance quality ISO
21940-11 grade G6.3. Five working days from the rotor being on their floor. They refuse
ATEX impellers, in-situ balancing, and any rotor with a cracked hub.
