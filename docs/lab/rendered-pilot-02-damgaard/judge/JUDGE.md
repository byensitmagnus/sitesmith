# Which of these two websites is the better finished website?

Two teams were given the same client brief and the same stack, and each built a site. Both are
finished and both are served. You have not been told anything about how either was built, and
you should not guess: nothing about the method is relevant to whether the result is good.

Read `BRIEF.md` first. It is the brief both teams worked from, word for word.

Then read `CHECKS.md`. The same external tools were run against both finished sites, by code
that did not come from either team.

Then look at every screenshot in `packet/`. They are full-page captures, taken by identical
code at the same widths, so neither candidate got a better photograph than the other.

## What you are judging

The client is a small Danish screed and drying firm with five employees, no photographs of
itself, and a specific problem: it gets calls every day from people it has to turn away. The
site exists so that the people it cannot help stop calling, and the people it can help book a
damp review without a phone call.

Judge the finished website as the client's customer would meet it, not as a document.

1. **Does it do the job the brief describes?** A visitor who is outside the area, or whose
   damage involves sewage, should find that out before they invest any effort. A visitor who
   is inside the area should be able to book and come away knowing what happens next.
2. **Are the facts right and honest?** The brief lists what this firm may and may not say
   about itself. A site that oversells is worse than a plain one, however handsome.
3. **Is it well designed?** Not decorated: designed. Does the layout carry the meaning, is the
   typography doing work, is the reading order the order a worried customer needs, does it
   hold together at 375 pixels as well as at 1440?
4. **Would you be comfortable handing it to this client?** One of these is the site you would
   ship. Say which and why.

Where the two differ, say what the difference costs the visitor in concrete terms.

## How to answer

Pick one. A tie is allowed only if you genuinely cannot separate them after looking, and then
you must say what would break the tie.

Be specific about what you saw. "Candidate 1's price table" is useful; "better hierarchy" is
not. If you criticise something, name the screenshot.

You will be asked for a structured verdict at the end. Nothing you write is shown to either
team, so write what you actually think.
