# Asset plan — borrowed credibility that was not lent

A logo wall is the strongest proof a page can carry and the easiest to fake. This one rests on
nothing: no named client in the evidence pack, and a stand-in in the manifest, which is a
fabricated endorsement whatever the row calls it.

## `client-logos`

- kind: proof
- carries: that four working yards buy their standing rigging here
- job: decide whether a yard of this size is a normal customer
- use: a four-up row above the enquiry form, marks at equal optical weight
- comparative: no
- without-it: the page claims yards buy here and shows nobody
- evidence: none

## `logo-primary`

- kind: brand
- carries: the ship's cleat the yard has used on its gate since 1904
- job: recognise the merchant across the dock and on an invoice
- use: header at 28px, footer at 20px
- comparative: no
- without-it: the page belongs to nobody in particular
- evidence: none — brand asset
