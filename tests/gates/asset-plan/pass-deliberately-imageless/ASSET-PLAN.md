# Asset plan — a page that is not carried by pictures

The direction declares imagery is not load-bearing and means it. The mark is the only asset,
and the typography does the work. That is the second door, taken deliberately.

## `logo-primary`

- kind: brand
- carries: the tally mark the cellar has chalked on casks since the desk was a book
- job: recognise the cellar desk on a printed consignment note
- use: header at 24px, and repeated once per cask in the count column
- comparative: no
- without-it: the page belongs to nobody in particular
- evidence: none — brand asset
