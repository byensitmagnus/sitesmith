# Asset plan — a rope merchant's counter

Every block carries an argument and names the job it serves. One asset is comparative, because
the visitor's job is to choose between five constructions.

## `rack-five-up`

- kind: product
- carries: that these are five different constructions, not five colours of one rope
- job: choose a construction for a named job
- use: one photograph, five ropes side by side at the same scale, above the spec table
- comparative: yes — five subjects in one frame, same lighting, same distance
- without-it: the page asserts the constructions differ and never shows it
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md "five stock constructions, bench shoot 2026-07-14"

## `splice-in-progress`

- kind: editorial
- carries: that a cut is finished by hand, which is why a cut length takes a day
- job: understand why an order is not dispatched the same afternoon
- use: half-column beside the lead-time paragraph, cropped to the hands
- comparative: no
- without-it: the lead time reads as a delay rather than as work
- evidence: EVIDENCE.md "bench shoot 2026-07-14, frames 31-38"

## `logo-primary`

- kind: brand
- carries: the ship's cleat the yard has used on its gate since 1904
- job: recognise the merchant across the dock and on an invoice
- use: header at 28px, footer at 20px, favicon derived
- comparative: no
- without-it: the page belongs to nobody in particular
- evidence: none — brand asset
