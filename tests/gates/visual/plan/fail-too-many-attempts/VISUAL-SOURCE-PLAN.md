# Visual source plan

## `bench-rope`

- role: the first thing a visitor sees, at the top of the page
- why: the trade is tactile and the page has no way to show it otherwise
- strategy: generate
- medium: photograph
- subject: three-strand polyester rope on an oak bench
- materials: polyester fibre; oak; galvanised steel
- composition: close, from just above, the lay running left to right
- lighting: north window, overcast, no fill
- aspect-ratios: 3:2; 4:5
- crops: 1440x960; 390x488
- focal: 48% 52%
- anti-references: harbour at sunset; nautical decor; a stock businessman
- must-not-change: the number of strands and the direction of the lay
- factual-risk: a wrong lay direction misrepresents the product a buyer is choosing
- max-attempts: 3
- candidates: 4
- capability: text-to-image
